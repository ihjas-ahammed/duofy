# Key to Flow: Journalism and News Reporting

**Subject Area:** `12_Communication_and_Media / 01_Journalism_and_News_Reporting`

---

## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
Journalism collects, verifies, and presents news, facts, and investigative reports, serving as a critical pillar of democratic accountability (The Fourth Estate).

---

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. The Core Journalism Toolkit
* **The Inverted Pyramid Structure:** Most important info (5 Ws & H: Who, What, Where, When, Why, How) $\to$ Supporting details $\to$ Background context.
* **Investigative Journalism:** Source verification, Freedom of Information (FOIA) requests, Data Journalism, Off-the-record / On-the-record ethics.
* **Journalistic Ethics:** Accuracy, Impartiality, Minimizing Harm, Avoiding Conflicts of Interest.

### 2. Top Recommended Resources
* **Journalism Bible:** *The Elements of Journalism* by Bill Kovach & Tom Rosenstiel.
* **Reporting Classic:** *News Reporting and Writing* by Melvin Mencher.

---

## 🎯 Problem-Solving Checklist
- [ ] Can you draft news stories adhering strictly to the Inverted Pyramid format?
- [ ] Can you navigate ethical dilemmas regarding confidential sources and privacy rights?
- [ ] Can you execute Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) requests for investigative data?
