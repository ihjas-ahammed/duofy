# Key to Flow: Public Relations and Strategic Communication

**Subject Area:** `12_Communication_and_Media / 03_Public_Relations_and_Strategic_Communication`

---

## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
Public Relations (PR) manages mutually beneficial relationships between organizations and their strategic publics through reputation management, crisis communication, and media relations.

---

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. The PR Management Process (RACE Model)
* **Research $\to$ Action $\to$ Communication $\to$ Evaluation.**
* **Crisis Communication (Coombs' Situational Crisis Communication Theory - SCCT):** Match crisis response strategy (Denial, Diminish, Rebuild, Bolster) to level of organizational crisis responsibility.
* **Media Relations:** Press releases, Media kits, Press conferences, Pitching stories.

### 2. Top Recommended Resources
* **PR Bible:** *Effective Public Relations* by Cutlip, Center, Broom.
* **Crisis Communication Classic:** *Ongoing Crisis Communication: Planning, Managing, and Responding* by W. Timothy Coombs.
