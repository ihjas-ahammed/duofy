# Key to Flow: Mass Communication and Media Theory

**Subject Area:** `12_Communication_and_Media / 02_Mass_Communication_and_Media_Theory`

---

## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
Mass Communication and Media Theory analyze how mass media (TV, radio, newspapers, digital platforms) shape public perception, cultural norms, political discourse, and societal behavior.

---

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. Major Media Theories
* **Agenda-Setting Theory (McCombs & Shaw):** Media doesn't tell people *what to think*, but *what to think about*.
* **Framing & Priming:** How media presentation influences audience interpretation.
* **Cultivation Theory (Gerbner):** Long-term television/media exposure shapes perception of reality ("Mean World Syndrome").
* **Uses and Gratifications Theory:** Active audience perspective (Why people seek specific media).
* **Media Ecology (Marshall McLuhan):** "The Medium is the Message."

### 2. Top Recommended Resources
* **Media Theory Classic:** *Mass Communication Theory* by Denis McQuail.
* **McLuhan Classic:** *Understanding Media: The Extensions of Man* by Marshall McLuhan.
* **Political Economy of Media:** *Manufacturing Consent* by Edward S. Herman & Noam Chomsky.
