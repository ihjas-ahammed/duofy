## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
**Vaccine Bioengineering, Checkpoint Blockade, CAR-T Cell Synthetic Immunology, and Tolerance Breakdown** govern modern clinical immunology, oncology therapeutics, and autoimmune pathology (Edward Jenner 1796, James Allison & Tasuku Honjo 2018 Nobel, Katalin Karikó & Drew Weissman 2023 Nobel):
* **Modern Vaccine Platforms & Synthetic mRNA Bioengineering:**
  - **The Karikó-Weissman Breakthrough (2023 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine):**
    - Unmodified in vitro transcribed (IVT) mRNA is recognized by endosomal TLR7/8 and TLR3, inducing catastrophic Type I interferon release and translation arrest via PKR and 2'-5'-OAS.
    - Substituting **$N1$-methylpseudouridine ($\text{m}1\Psi$)** for natural uridine completely abolishes TLR activation while increasing mRNA stability and translational efficiency tenfold!
  - **Lipid Nanoparticles (LNPs):** 4-component lipid formulation (Ionizable cationic lipid [e.g. ALC-0315, SM-102: positively charged at acidic endosomal pH $\approx 5.5$ to promote endosomal escape, neutral at physiological pH $7.4$], PEGylated lipid [prevents aggregation], Phospholipid [DSPC], and Cholesterol).
  - **Vaccine Spectrum:** Live-attenuated (MMR, Sabin polio) $\implies$ Inactivated (Salk polio) $\implies$ Subunit/Toxoid (Tetanus, Diphtheria) $\implies$ Recombinant VLP (HPV Gardasil) $\implies$ Viral vector (ChAdOx1) $\implies$ mRNA-LNP (BNT162b2, mRNA-1273).
* **Immune Checkpoint Blockade Immunotherapy (2018 Nobel Prize):**
  - **CTLA-4 Blockade (James Allison - Ipilimumab):**
    - CTLA-4 is an inhibitory homolog of CD28 expressed on activated T cells and Tregs. It binds B7-1/B7-2 with $\approx 20-50\times$ higher affinity than CD28, outcompeting costimulation and stripping B7 via trans-endocytosis.
    - Anti-CTLA-4 mAb (*Ipilimumab*) uncouples the inhibitory brake in lymph nodes, driving massive anti-tumor T-cell priming!
  - **PD-1 / PD-L1 Blockade (Tasuku Honjo - Pembrolizumab, Nivolumab, Atezolizumab):**
    - PD-1 expressed on exhausted tumor-infiltrating lymphocytes (TILs) binds PD-L1/PD-L2 on tumor cells/macrophages $\to$ recruits **SHP-2 phosphatase** $\to$ dephosphorylates CD28 and TCR signaling intermediates.
    - Anti-PD-1/PD-L1 mAbs restore cytotoxic killing in the tumor microenvironment!
* **Chimeric Antigen Receptor (CAR) T-Cell Synthetic Biology:**
  - **Modular CAR Architecture:**
    1. **Ectodomain:** Single-chain variable fragment (**scFv**) derived from a monoclonal antibody (e.g. anti-CD19) providing HLA-independent direct target binding.
    2. **Hinge & Transmembrane Domain:** Flexible spacer (CD8$\alpha$ or IgG Fc).
    3. **Endodomain:** Dual intracellular signaling domains:
       - **Costimulatory Domain:** **4-1BB (CD137)** (promotes mitochondrial biogenesis and long-term memory persistence) or **CD28** (drives rapid, intense effector glycolysis).
       - **Primary Activation Domain:** **$\text{CD3}\zeta$** bearing 3 ITAM motifs.
  - **Toxicities:** **Cytokine Release Syndrome (CRS)** driven by macrophage IL-6 / IL-1 hyperactivation (treated with the IL-6 receptor antagonist **Tocilizumab**), and ICANS neurotoxicity.
* **Central & Peripheral Immune Tolerance and Autoimmunity:**
  - **Thymic Central Tolerance & AIRE:** The **Autoimmune Regulator (AIRE)** transcription factor drives promiscuous expression of tissue-restricted self-antigens (TRAs: insulin, thyroglobulin) in **Medullary Thymic Epithelial Cells (mTECs)**. Developing T cells with high-affinity TCRs for self-peptide-MHC undergo clonal deletion (negative selection). *AIRE* loss-of-function $\implies$ **APECED / APS-1 (Autoimmune Polyendocrinopathy-Candidiasis-Ectodermal Dystrophy)**.
  - **The 4 Coombs and Gell Hypersensitivity Reactions:**
    - **Type I (Immediate):** IgE cross-linking on mast cells $\to$ Histamine (Anaphylaxis, Asthma).
    - **Type II (Cytotoxic Antibody):** IgG/IgM binding cell-surface antigens $\to$ complement/ADCC (Myasthenia Gravis, Goodpasture, Graves).
    - **Type III (Immune Complex):** Circulating Ag-Ab complexes depositing in capillaries $\to$ vasculitis (SLE Lupus, Serum Sickness).
    - **Type IV (Delayed-Type Hypersensitivity - DTH):** Sensitized CD4+ (Th1) and CD8+ T cells $\to$ Macrophage activation (Poison Ivy contact dermatitis, PPD Mantoux tuberculin test, Multiple Sclerosis).
