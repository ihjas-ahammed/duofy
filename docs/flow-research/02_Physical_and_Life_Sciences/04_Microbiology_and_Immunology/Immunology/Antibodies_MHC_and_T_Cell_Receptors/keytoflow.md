## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
**Somatic Recombination, Antigen Processing & Presentation, and Lymphocyte Receptor Signaling** govern how the vertebrate adaptive immune system generates astronomical molecular diversity and distinguishes self from non-self (Susumu Tonegawa 1987 Nobel, Peter Doherty & Rolf Zinkernagel 1996 Nobel, Janeway):
* **Antibody (Immunoglobulin) Architecture & Isotypes:**
  - **Structure:** Heterotetramer of $2$ identical Heavy ($H$) chains and $2$ identical Light ($L$) chains held by interchain disulfide bonds:
    - **Fab (Fragment antigen-binding):** Formed by $V_H + C_{H1}$ and $V_L + C_L$; contains the antigen-binding site shaped by 3 hypervariable **Complementarity-Determining Regions (CDR1, CDR2, CDR3)**.
    - **Fc (Fragment crystallizable):** Formed by paired $C_{H2} + C_{H3}$ (and $C_{H4}$); binds host $\text{Fc}\gamma\text{R}$, $\text{Fc}\epsilon\text{RI}$, and C1q complement.
  - **The 5 Isotypes:**
    - **IgM:** Monomer on naive B cells; pentamer with **J-chain** in serum; premier primary activator of the classical complement cascade.
    - **IgD:** B-cell receptor co-expressed with IgM via alternative RNA splicing.
    - **IgG (IgG1-4):** Most abundant serum Ig; crosses human placenta (neonatal FcRn); powerful opsonin and mediator of ADCC.
    - **IgA:** Monomer in blood; **secretory dimer** with J-chain and Secretory Component in saliva, tears, breast milk, and gut mucosa.
    - **IgE:** Binds high-affinity **$\text{Fc}\epsilon\text{RI}$** on mast cells and basophils $\implies$ degranulation in type I hypersensitivity and anti-helminth immunity.
* **V(D)J Somatic Recombination (Susumu Tonegawa, 1987 Nobel):**
  - **The Molecular Machine:**
    - **RAG-1 and RAG-2:** Lymphoid-specific recombinases that bind **Recombination Signal Sequences (RSS: heptamer-spacer-nonamer)**.
    - **The 12/23 Rule:** A gene segment flanked by a $12\text{-bp spacer}$ can join *only* to a segment flanked by a $23\text{-bp spacer}$, enforcing correct $V \to D \to J$ ordering!
    - **Artemis Endonuclease:** Opens hairpins generated by RAG cleavage, creating **P-nucleotides** (palindromic).
    - **Terminal Deoxynucleotidyl Transferase (TdT):** Randomly adds non-templated **N-nucleotides** to the free $3'$ ends, maximizing **Junctional Diversity** in the hypervariable CDR3 loop!
* **Major Histocompatibility Complex (MHC / HLA):**
  - **MHC Class I (Endogenous / Cytosolic Pathway $\to$ CD8+ Cytotoxic T Cells):**
    - **Structure:** Heavy $\alpha$ chain ($\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3$) non-covalently associated with **$\beta_2\text{-microglobulin}$**; closed binding groove accommodating short **$8-10\text{ aa}$ peptides**.
    - **Pathway:** Cytosolic viral/tumor proteins degraded by the **Immunoproteasome** $\to$ Peptides pumped into ER lumen by **TAP1/TAP2 transporters** $\to$ Loaded onto nascent MHC I via tapasin $\to$ Exported to plasma membrane of **all nucleated cells**.
  - **MHC Class II (Exogenous / Endocytic Pathway $\to$ CD4+ Helper T Cells):**
    - **Structure:** Heterodimer of $\alpha$ chain ($\alpha_1, \alpha_2$) and $\beta$ chain ($\beta_1, \beta_2$); open binding groove accommodating long **$13-25\text{ aa}$ peptides**.
    - **Pathway:** Extracellular pathogens internalized by phagocytosis $\to$ Nascent MHC II assembled in ER with **Invariant Chain (Ii)** blocking peptide groove $\to$ Invariant chain degraded in endosome leaving **CLIP** peptide $\to$ **HLA-DM** exchanges CLIP for high-affinity foreign peptide $\to$ Presented on **professional APCs (Dendritic cells, Macrophages, B cells)**.
* **T-Cell Receptor (TCR) & MHC Restriction (Doherty & Zinkernagel, 1996 Nobel):**
  - **TCR Complex:** $\alpha\beta$ (or $\gamma\delta$) clonotypic heterodimer associated with invariant **CD3 signaling chains ($\gamma, \delta, \epsilon, \zeta$)** bearing cytoplasmic **ITAMs (Immunoreceptor Tyrosine-based Activation Motifs)**.
  - **MHC Restriction:** A T-cell recognizes foreign peptide *only* when presented in the context of the host's own self-MHC molecule (learned via positive selection in the thymus).

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. The Antigen Processing Factory & 12/23 Ruler
* MHC I = Endogenous/Inside $\to$ Proteasome $\to$ TAP $\to$ ER $\to$ CD8+. ($1 \times 8 = 8$).
* MHC II = Exogenous/Outside $\to$ Endosome $\to$ CLIP/HLA-DM $\to$ CD4+. ($2 \times 4 = 8$).
