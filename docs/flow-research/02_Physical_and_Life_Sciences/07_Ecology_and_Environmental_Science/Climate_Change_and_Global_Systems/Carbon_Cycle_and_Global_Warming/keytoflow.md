## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
**Biogeochemical Carbon Reservoirs, Anthropogenic Airborne Fraction, Logarithmic Radiative Forcing, Nonlinear Climate Feedbacks, and Transient Cumulative Carbon Budgets** govern the thermodynamic state and long-term trajectory of Earth's climate system (Svante Arrhenius 1896, Charles David Keeling 1958, Syukuro Manabe 1967, IPCC AR6 2021):
* **The Global Carbon Cycle & Reservoirs:**
  - **Carbon Pools / Reservoirs:**
    - **Atmosphere:** $\approx 875\text{ GtC}$ ($425\text{ ppm}$; pre-industrial was $590\text{ GtC} / 280\text{ ppm}$).
    - **Terrestrial Biosphere & Soils:** $\approx 2500\text{ GtC}$ (vegetation $\sim 500\text{ GtC}$, soil organic matter $\sim 2000\text{ GtC}$).
    - **Global Ocean:** $\approx 38,000\text{ GtC}$ (dissolved inorganic carbon, carbonate buffer).
    - **Lithosphere & Fossil Reserves:** $>100,000,000\text{ GtC}$ (sedimentary carbonate rocks & recoverable fossil fuels $\sim 4000\text{ GtC}$).
  - **Anthropogenic Emissions & Partitioning ($10-11\text{ GtC/year} \approx 38-40\text{ GtCO}_2\text{/year}$):**
    - **Airborne Fraction ($AF \approx 45-48\%$):** Fraction of human emissions remaining in the atmosphere ($\sim 5\text{ GtC/yr}$), recorded by the **Keeling Curve** at Mauna Loa.
    - **Ocean Sink ($\approx 25-28\%$):** Dissolution via Henry's law and the marine carbonate system ($\sim 3\text{ GtC/yr}$), driving ocean acidification.
    - **Terrestrial Land Sink ($\approx 28-30\%$):** Enhanced $\text{CO}_2$ fertilization of forests and vegetation regrowth ($\sim 3.2\text{ GtC/yr}$).
* **Radiative Forcing & Equilibrium Climate Sensitivity (ECS):**
  - **The Logarithmic Radiative Forcing Formula (Myhre et al. 1998; IPCC):**
    $$\mathbf{\Delta F = 5.35 \ln\left(\frac{C}{C_0}\right) \text{ W/m}^2}$$
    - For a doubling of atmospheric $\text{CO}_2$ ($2 \times \text{CO}_2$, e.g. $280 \to 560\text{ ppm}$):
      $$\mathbf{\Delta F_{2\times\text{CO}_2} = 5.35 \ln(2) \approx 3.71\text{ W/m}^2}$$
  - **Equilibrium Climate Sensitivity (ECS):**
    $$\mathbf{\Delta T_{\text{eq}} = \lambda \Delta F = \frac{\Delta F_{2\times\text{CO}_2}}{\lambda_{\text{net}}} \approx 2.5 - 4.0^\circ\text{C} \quad (\text{Best estimate } 3.0^\circ\text{C})}$$
    *(Without feedbacks, bare Planck blackbody response is only $\Delta T_0 \approx 1.2^\circ\text{C}$; positive feedbacks amplify warming by $>2.5\times$!)*.
* **Climate Feedbacks (The Earth System Multipliers):**
  - **The Feedback Equation:** $\lambda_{\text{net}} = \lambda_0 + \sum \lambda_i$ (where Planck response $\lambda_0 \approx -3.2\text{ W}\cdot\text{m}^{-2}\text{K}^{-1}$).
  - **1. Water Vapor Feedback ($\lambda_{\text{wv}} \approx +1.8\text{ W}\cdot\text{m}^{-2}\text{K}^{-1}$ - The Largest Positive Feedback):**
    - Warmer troposphere holds exponentially more water vapor via **Clausius-Clapeyron relation ($\approx +7\%\text{ moisture per } 1^\circ\text{C}$ warming)** $\implies$ Water vapor is a potent greenhouse gas that doubles the initial warming!
  - **2. Ice-Albedo Feedback ($\lambda_{\text{ice}} \approx +0.3\text{ W}\cdot\text{m}^{-2}\text{K}^{-1}$):** Melting of reflective polar sea ice ($\alpha \approx 0.6-0.8$) exposes dark open ocean ($\alpha \approx 0.06$), absorbing more solar shortwave radiation.
  - **3. Permafrost Carbon Feedback:** Thawing of Arctic permafrost releases ancient organic carbon as $\text{CO}_2$ and $\text{CH}_4$.
  - **4. Planck Radiation Feedback ($\lambda_0 < 0$ - The Fundamental Stabilizer):** Stefan-Boltzmann law $E = \sigma T^4$ radiates thermal infrared directly into space as planet warms.
* **Transient Climate Response to Cumulative Emissions (TCRE):**
  - **Linearity of Global Warming with Cumulative Carbon:**
    $$\mathbf{\Delta T(t) \approx \text{TCRE} \times E_{\text{cum}} \qquad (\text{TCRE} \approx 0.45^\circ\text{C} \pm 0.1^\circ\text{C} \text{ per } 1000\text{ GtCO}_2)}$$
  - Enables the calculation of a finite **Remaining Carbon Budget** to limit global temperature rise to $1.5^\circ\text{C}$ or $2.0^\circ\text{C}$.
