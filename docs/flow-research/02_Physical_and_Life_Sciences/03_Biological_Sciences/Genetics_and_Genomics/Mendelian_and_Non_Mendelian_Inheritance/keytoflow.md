## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
**Mendelian Genetics, Linkage Mapping, Epistatic Interactions, and Non-Mendelian Epigenetic Inheritance** govern the transmission of hereditary information across generations (Gregor Mendel 1865, Thomas Hunt Morgan 1911, Alfred Sturtevant):
* **Classical Mendelian Principles:**
  - **Mendel's First Law (Segregation):** The two alleles for each gene segregate during gamete formation (meiosis I anaphase) so that each gamete carries only one allele ($3:1$ phenotypic ratio in monohybrid $F_2$, $1:2:1$ genotypic ratio).
  - **Mendel's Second Law (Independent Assortment):** Alleles of unlinked genes assort independently into gametes ($9:3:3:1$ phenotypic ratio in dihybrid $F_2$).
* **Gene Interactions & Epistatic Ratio Modifications (Dihybrid 16-Grid):**
  - **Recessive Epistasis ($9:3:4$):** Homozygous recessive at one locus ($bb$) masks the phenotypic expression of the other locus (e.g. Labrador retriever coat color: $B\_E\_$ Black, $bbE\_$ Brown/Chocolate, $\_\_ee$ Yellow).
  - **Dominant Epistasis ($12:3:1$):** Dominant allele at one locus ($A\_$) masks the other locus (e.g. summer squash fruit color).
  - **Complementary / Duplicate Recessive Epistasis ($9:7$):** Dominant alleles at *both* loci ($A\_B\_$) are required to produce functional phenotype; homozygous recessive at either locus ($aa$ or $bb$) yields mutant phenotype (e.g. sweet pea purple vs white flower color).
  - **Duplicate Dominant Epistasis ($15:1$):** Dominant allele at *either* locus produces the phenotype (e.g. shepherd's purse triangular vs ovoid seed capsules).
* **Genetic Linkage, Recombination, and Three-Point Mapping:**
  - **Recombination Frequency (RF):**
    $$\text{RF} = \frac{\text{Number of Recombinant Progeny}}{\text{Total Progeny}} \times 100\% = \text{Map Distance in Centimorgans (cM / m.u.)}$$
  - **Three-Point Testcross ($AaBbCc \times aabbcc$):**
    1. **Parental Classes:** The two most frequent phenotypic classes (non-crossover).
    2. **Double Crossover (DCO) Classes:** The two rarest phenotypic classes.
    3. **Gene Order Determination:** The single gene that "switches" between parental and DCO classes is the **middle gene**!
    4. **Interference & Coefficient of Coincidence ($C$):** $C = \frac{\text{Observed DCO}}{\text{Expected DCO}}$, $\text{Interference } I = 1 - C$.
* **Non-Mendelian Epigenetic Inheritance & Imprinting:**
  - **Genomic Imprinting:** Monoallelic gene expression dictated by **parent-of-origin DNA methylation** established during gametogenesis (e.g. Chromosome 15q11-q13 deletion):
    - **Paternal Deletion $\implies$ Prader-Willi Syndrome (PWS)** (maternal copy is naturally imprinted/silenced).
    - **Maternal Deletion $\implies$ Angelman Syndrome (AS)** (paternal copy is naturally imprinted/silenced; *UBE3A* loss).
  - **Mitochondrial Maternal Inheritance:** Non-Mendelian cytoplasmic transmission (all children of affected mother inherit disorder; fathers never transmit; e.g. Leber's Hereditary Optic Neuropathy [LHON]).

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. The 16-Grid Collapse & DCO Triad Method
* For epistasis: Group the 16 squares of the standard $9:3:3:1$ grid into $(9), (3), (3), (1)$ and sum identical phenotypes.
* For three-point mapping: Highest pair = Parentals, Lowest pair = DCOs, Mismatched middle allele = Central gene!
