## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
**Animal Physiology and Neurobiology** integrate cellular biophysics, electrophysiology, neuromuscular contractility, and systemic organ-level homeostatic regulation:
* **Membrane Biophysics & Action Potential Dynamics:**
  - **Nernst Equilibrium Potential:** $E_{\text{ion}} = \frac{RT}{zF} \ln\left(\frac{[\text{ion}]_{\text{out}}}{[\text{ion}]_{\text{in}}}\right) = \frac{61.5\text{ mV}}{z} \log_{10}\left(\frac{[\text{ion}]_{\text{out}}}{[\text{ion}]_{\text{in}}}\right) \quad (\text{at } 37^\circ\text{C})$.
  - **Goldman-Hodgkin-Katz (GHK) Voltage Equation:**
    $$V_m = \frac{RT}{F} \ln\left( \frac{P_{\text{K}}[\text{K}^+]_{\text{out}} + P_{\text{Na}}[\text{Na}^+]_{\text{out}} + P_{\text{Cl}}[\text{Cl}^-]_{\text{in}}}{P_{\text{K}}[\text{K}^+]_{\text{in}} + P_{\text{Na}}[\text{Na}^+]_{\text{in}} + P_{\text{Cl}}[\text{Cl}^-]_{\text{out}}} \right)$$
  - **Hodgkin-Huxley Cycle (Action Potential Phases):**
    1. **Resting State ($-70\text{ mV}$):** Dominated by $P_{\text{K}} \gg P_{\text{Na}}$ (leak $\text{K}^+$ channels).
    2. **Depolarization Phase (Threshold $-55\text{ mV} \to +30\text{ mV}$):** Rapid opening of voltage-gated $\text{Na}^+$ channels ($P_{\text{Na}} \gg P_{\text{K}}$).
    3. **Repolarization Phase:** Inactivation of $\text{Na}^+$ channels (ball-and-chain inactivation gate) and delayed opening of voltage-gated $\text{K}^+$ channels.
    4. **Hyperpolarization & Refractory Period:** Absolute (inactivated $\text{Na}^+$ channels) vs Relative (open $\text{K}^+$ channels).
    5. **Saltatory Conduction:** Action potential jumps rapidly between Nodes of Ranvier along myelinated axons ($v \propto \text{diameter}$, up to $120\text{ m/s}$).
* **Synaptic Transmission & Neurochemistry:**
  - Action potential reaches axon terminal $\implies$ Opening of Voltage-Gated $\text{Ca}^{2+}$ Channels.
  - $\text{Ca}^{2+}$ influx binds **Synaptotagmin**, triggering the **SNARE Complex (Syntaxin, SNAP-25, Synaptobrevin/VAMP)** to mediate vesicle fusion and quantal exocytosis of neurotransmitters (Acetylcholine, Glutamate, GABA).
  - Postsynaptic potentials: **EPSP** ($\text{Na}^+/\text{Ca}^{2+}$ influx depolarizes) vs **IPSP** ($\text{Cl}^-$ influx / $\text{K}^+$ efflux hyperpolarizes).
* **Neuromuscular Mechanics & The Sliding Filament Theory (Huxley & Hanson, 1954):**
  - Motor neuron AP $\to$ ACh release at NMJ $\to$ End-plate potential $\to$ T-tubule depolarization $\to$ DHPR (Dihydropyridine receptor) activates RyR (Ryanodine receptor), releasing $\text{Ca}^{2+}$ from the Sarcoplasmic Reticulum.
  - $\text{Ca}^{2+}$ binds **Troponin C**, causing **Tropomyosin** to shift away from the active binding sites on the Actin thin filament.
  - **The Cross-Bridge Cycle:**
    1. **Attached State (Rigor):** Myosin head bound to actin with no ATP.
    2. **Detachment:** ATP binds myosin head, causing detachment from actin.
    3. **Cocking / Hydrolysis:** ATP hydrolyzes to $\text{ADP} + \text{P}_i$, swinging myosin into high-energy $90^\circ$ cocked state.
    4. **Cross-Bridge Formation:** Myosin binds new actin subunit.
    5. **Power Stroke:** $\text{P}_i$ release triggers the mechanical power stroke ($45^\circ$ swivel), pulling actin filament toward sarcomere M-line ($10\text{ nm}$ displacement), followed by ADP release!
* **Renal Physiology & Osmoregulation:**
  - **Countercurrent Multiplier in Loop of Henle:**
    - **Descending Limb:** Permeable to $\text{H}_2\text{O}$ via AQP-1 aquaporins; impermeable to $\text{NaCl} \implies$ Tubular fluid concentrates up to $1200\text{ mOsm/kg}$ at hairpin turn.
    - **Ascending Limb:** Impermeable to $\text{H}_2\text{O}$; actively pumps $\text{Na}^+/\text{K}^+/2\text{Cl}^-$ ($\text{NKCC2}$ cotransporter) into medullary interstitium $\implies$ Generates hyperosmotic corticomedullary gradient.
  - **Hormonal Osmoregulation:**
    - **Antidiuretic Hormone (ADH / Vasopressin):** Inserts Aquaporin-2 (AQP-2) into collecting duct apical membrane $\implies$ Concentrated urine.
    - **Renin-Angiotensin-Aldosterone System (RAAS):** Low renal perfusion $\to$ Renin $\to$ Angiotensin II $\to$ Aldosterone $\to$ $\text{Na}^+$ reabsorption in DCT.

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. The Homeostatic Flowsheet Method
* Map every physiological regulatory loop as: Stimulus $\to$ Sensor $\to$ Afferent pathway $\to$ Integrating Center $\to$ Efferent pathway $\to$ Effector $\to$ Response $\to$ Negative feedback shutdown!
