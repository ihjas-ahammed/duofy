# Key to Flow: Biological Sciences

**Subject Area:** `02_Physical_and_Life_Sciences / 03_Biological_Sciences`

---

## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
Biological Sciences explore living organisms from molecular machinery to whole-organism physiology and evolutionary diversification. The **Central Dogma of Molecular Biology** governs information flow:
$$\text{DNA} \xrightarrow{\text{Transcription}} \text{RNA} \xrightarrow{\text{Translation}} \text{Protein}$$

---

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. Molecular & Cell Biology Mechanics
* **DNA Replication:** Helicase, Primase, DNA Polymerase III ($\text{5'} \to \text{3'}$ direction), Okazaki fragments.
* **Gene Regulation:** Lac Operon, Transcription Factors, Epigenetics (DNA methylation, Histone acetylation).
* **Cell Cycle Control:** Cyclins and Cyclin-Dependent Kinases (CDKs), G1/S and G2/M checkpoints, p53 tumor suppressor.

### 2. Genetics & Evolution
* **Mendelian & Population Genetics:** Punnett Squares, Hardy-Weinberg Equilibrium ($p^2 + 2pq + q^2 = 1$).
* **Molecular Evolution & Phylogenetics:** Maximum Parsimony, Neighbor-Joining trees, Molecular Clocks.

### 3. Top Recommended Resources
* **The Gold Standard Molecular Text:** *Molecular Biology of the Cell* by Alberts et al.
* **Comprehensive General Biology:** *Campbell Biology* by Urry et al.
* **Genetics Standard:** *Introduction to Genetic Analysis* by Griffiths et al.
