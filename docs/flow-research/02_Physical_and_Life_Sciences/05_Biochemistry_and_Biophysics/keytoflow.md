# Key to Flow: Biochemistry and Biophysics

**Subject Area:** `02_Physical_and_Life_Sciences / 05_Biochemistry_and_Biophysics`

---

## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
Biochemistry explores chemical processes within living organisms, focusing on enzyme dynamics and energy transformation. Biophysics applies physical principles (thermodynamics, mechanics, optics) to understand biomolecular structures and functions.

---

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. Enzyme Kinetics & Thermodynamics
* **Michaelis-Menten Model:** $v_0 = \frac{V_{\max} [S]}{K_m + [S]}$.
  * $K_m$: Substrate concentration at half-maximal velocity (measures affinity).
  * $k_{\text{cat}}$: Turnover number.
* **Enzyme Inhibition:**
  * **Competitive:** Increases $K_m$, unchanged $V_{\max}$ (V-shaped Lineweaver-Burk plot intersection on y-axis).
  * **Non-Competitive:** Decreases $V_{\max}$, unchanged $K_m$.
  * **Uncompetitive:** Decreases both $K_m$ and $V_{\max}$.

### 2. Metabolic Engine
* **Glycolysis $\to$ Pyruvate Dehydrogenase $\to$ Krebs Cycle $\to$ Electron Transport Chain (ETC).**
* **Proton Motive Force ($\Delta p$):** $\text{pH}$ and electrical gradient driving ATP Synthase ($F_0 F_1$ complex).

### 3. Biophysical Techniques
* **X-ray Crystallography & Cryo-EM:** Atomic resolution structural determination.
* **Single-Molecule Biophysics:** Optical Tweezers, Atomic Force Microscopy (AFM), FRET (Förster Resonance Energy Transfer).

### 4. Top Recommended Resources
* **The Gold Standard Biochemistry Text:** *Lehninger Principles of Biochemistry* by Nelson & Cox.
* **Biophysics Classic:** *Physical Biology of the Cell* by Phillips, Kondev, Theriot, and Garcia.
* **Enzyme Kinetics Focus:** *Enzymes: Principles and Biotechnology* by Palmer & Bonner.
