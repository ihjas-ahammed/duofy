## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
**Paleomagnetic Seafloor Spreading, Lithospheric Boundary Kinematics, Geodynamic Slab Pull/Ridge Push Mechanics, and The Wilson Supercontinent Cycle** govern the structural, seismic, and evolutionary dynamics of planetary lithospheres (Alfred Wegener 1912, Harry Hess 1962, Fred Vine & Drummond Matthews 1963, J. Tuzo Wilson 1965):
* **From Continental Drift to Seafloor Spreading:**
  - **Wegener's Paradigm (1912):** Pangaea supercontinent supported by fossil matching (*Mesosaurus, Glossopteris, Lystrosaurus*), continental shelf fit (Bullard $500\text{-fathom}$ fit), matching Appalachian-Caledonian mountain belts, and late Paleozoic Karoo glacial striations.
  - **The Seafloor Spreading Hypothesis (Harry Hess 1962):** Magma upwells at mid-ocean ridges (MORs), creating new oceanic crust that moves outward like a conveyor belt, sinking back into the mantle at deep-sea subduction trenches.
  - **The Vine-Matthews-Morley (VMM) Hypothesis (1963):**
    - The seafloor operates as a natural **magnetic tape recorder**:
      - Basaltic magma cools below the **Curie Temperature of Magnetite ($\approx 580^\circ\text{C}$)** $\to$ locks in the current geomagnetic field orientation (**Thermoremanent Magnetization [TRM]**).
      - Periodic geomagnetic field reversals (Normal [Brunhes] vs Reversed [Matuyama]) combined with symmetric seafloor spreading produce **mirror-image "zebra stripe" marine magnetic anomalies** on opposite sides of mid-ocean ridges!
* **Plate Boundary Kinematics & Fault Geometries:**
  $$\begin{array}{|l|l|l|l|}
  \hline
  \textbf{Boundary Type} & \textbf{Relative Motion} & \textbf{Tectonic Setting \& Examples} & \textbf{Dominant Fault / Volcanism} \\
  \hline
  \textbf{Divergent} & \text{Extension / Pull apart} & \text{Mid-Atlantic Ridge, East African Rift} & \text{Normal faults; Decompression basaltic volcanism} \\
  \textbf{Convergent (O-C)} & \text{Subduction (Dense Ocean under Continent)} & \text{Cascadia, Andes, Japan} & \text{Megathrust faults; Intermediate/felsic arc volcanism} \\
  \textbf{Convergent (O-O)} & \text{Subduction (Older denser ocean under younger)} & \text{Mariana Trench, Aleutian Islands} & \text{Deep trenches; Island arc volcanism} \\
  \textbf{Convergent (C-C)} & \text{Collisional Orogeny (Buoyant crust)} & \text{Himalayas, Zagros, Alps} & \text{Thrust/Reverse faults; Crustal thickening; No volcanism} \\
  \textbf{Transform} & \text{Horizontal strike-slip shear} & \text{San Andreas Fault, Alpine Fault} & \text{Strike-slip faults; Shallow seismicity; No volcanism} \\
  \hline
  \end{array}$$
* **Geodynamic Driving Forces:**
  - **1. Slab Pull ($\approx 90\%$ of total force - The Dominant Engine):**
    - As oceanic lithosphere moves away from the ridge, it cools conductively, thickens, and becomes denser than the underlying warm asthenosphere.
    - Upon subduction, dehydration and metamorphic phase transition of basalt/gabbro into **dense Eclogite ($\rho \approx 3.5-3.6\text{ g/cm}^3$)** provides immense **negative buoyancy**, acting as a heavy sinking anchor that pulls the trailing plate!
  - **2. Ridge Push:** Gravitational sliding force ($F_{\text{RP}} = g \Delta \rho \alpha T_m \kappa t$) exerted down the elevated bathymetric slope of the mid-ocean ridge ($2.5\text{ km}$ deep) toward older, deeper ocean basins ($5-6\text{ km}$ deep).
  - **3. Mantle Drag / Basal Traction:** Viscous coupling between mantle convection cells and the lithospheric base.
* **The Wilson Cycle of Ocean Basin Evolution (J. Tuzo Wilson 1966):**
  $$\begin{array}{|c|l|l|l|}
  \hline
  \textbf{Stage} & \textbf{Wilson Cycle Stage} & \textbf{Tectonic Motion} & \textbf{Modern Geological Example} \\
  \hline
  \mathbf{1} & \textbf{Embryonic} & \text{Continental crustal rifting and thermal doming} & \text{East African Rift Valley} \\
  \mathbf{2} & \textbf{Juvenile} & \text{Initial seafloor spreading; narrow proto-ocean} & \text{Red Sea, Gulf of California} \\
  \mathbf{3} & \textbf{Mature} & \text{Broad ocean basin with wide passive margins} & \text{Atlantic Ocean} \\
  \mathbf{4} & \textbf{Declining} & \text{Subduction initiation; contracting basin with arcs} & \text{Pacific Ocean} \\
  \mathbf{5} & \textbf{Terminal} & \text{Narrowing basin undergoing collision} & \text{Mediterranean Sea, Persian Gulf} \\
  \mathbf{6} & \textbf{Suturing / Relict Scar} & \text{Continental collision; mountain building} & \textbf{Himalayas (Indus-Yarlung Suture)} \\
  \hline
  \end{array}$$
