## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
**Silicate Crystal Chemistry, Bowen's Magmatic Differentiation, Sedimentary Diagenesis, and Metamorphic P-T Facies Trajectories** govern the chemical composition, thermodynamic stability, and lithological evolution of planetary crusts (Norman L. Bowen 1928, George Barrow 1893, Pentti Eskola 1915):
* **Silicate Mineral Classification & Tetrahedral Polymerization:**
  - Fundamental building block: **Silica Tetrahedron ($[\text{SiO}_4]^{4-}$)**, where $\text{Si}^{4+}$ sits in tetrahedral coordination with four $\text{O}^{2-}$ ions.
  - **The 6 Structural Subclasses (Increasing Oxygen Sharing & Si:O Ratio):**
    $$\begin{array}{|l|l|l|c|l|}
    \hline
    \textbf{Silicate Class} & \textbf{Structural Motif} & \textbf{Bridging Oxygens} & \mathbf{\text{Si:O Ratio}} & \textbf{Classic Mineral Examples} \\
    \hline
    \textbf{Nesosilicates (Ortho)} & \text{Isolated } [\text{SiO}_4]^{4-} \text{ tetrahedra} & 0 & 1:4 & \text{Olivine } (\text{Mg,Fe})_2\text{SiO}_4\text{, Garnet, Zircon, } \text{Al}_2\text{SiO}_5 \\
    \textbf{Sorosilicates (Di)} & \text{Paired } [\text{Si}_2\text{O}_7]^{6-} \text{ groups} & 1 & 2:7 & \text{Epidote, Lawsonite} \\
    \textbf{Cyclosilicates (Ring)} & \text{Closed rings } [\text{Si}_6\text{O}_{18}]^{12-} & 2 & 1:3 & \text{Beryl (Emerald), Tourmaline} \\
    \textbf{Inosilicates (Single Chain)} & \text{Continuous 1D single chains} & 2 & 1:3 & \textbf{Pyroxenes} \ (\text{Augite, Enstatite, Diopside}) \\
    \textbf{Inosilicates (Double Chain)} & \text{Interconnected 1D double chains} & 2 \text{ and } 3 & 4:11 & \textbf{Amphiboles} \ (\text{Hornblende, Tremolite, Actinolite}) \\
    \textbf{Phyllosilicates (Sheet)} & \text{2D hexagonal sheets } [\text{Si}_4\text{O}_{10}]^{4-} & 3 & 2:5 & \textbf{Micas} \ (\text{Biotite, Muscovite})\text{, Clays, Talc, Serpentine} \\
    \textbf{Tectosilicates (Framework)} & \text{3D fully linked network} & 4 \text{ (All)} & 1:2 & \textbf{Quartz } (\text{SiO}_2)\text{, Feldspars (Plagioclase, K-Feldspar)} \\
    \hline
    \end{array}$$
* **Bowen's Reaction Series & Igneous Petrology (Norman L. Bowen 1928):**
  - Models the progressive crystallization of silicate minerals from a cooling basaltic magma:
    - **Discontinuous Branch (Mafic Iron-Magnesium Silicates):**
      $$\mathbf{\text{Olivine } (1400^\circ\text{C}) \longrightarrow \text{Pyroxene} \longrightarrow \text{Amphibole} \longrightarrow \text{Biotite Mica}}$$
      *(Each step reacts with remaining melt to increase tetrahedral polymerization from isolated $\to$ chain $\to$ double chain $\to$ sheet)*.
    - **Continuous Branch (Felsic Plagioclase Solid Solution):**
      $$\mathbf{\text{Ca-rich Anorthite } (\text{CaAl}_2\text{Si}_2\text{O}_8) \longrightarrow \text{Na-rich Albite } (\text{NaAlSi}_3\text{O}_8)}$$
    - **Residual Low-Temperature Melt ($600-750^\circ\text{C}$):**
      $$\mathbf{\text{Potassium Feldspar (Orthoclase)} \longrightarrow \text{Muscovite Mica} \longrightarrow \text{Quartz}}$$
  - **Igneous Rock Classification Matrix:**
    - **Ultramafic:** Peridotite (Plutonic) / Komatiite (Volcanic).
    - **Mafic:** Gabbro (Plutonic, phaneritic) / Basalt (Volcanic, aphanitic).
    - **Intermediate:** Diorite (Plutonic) / Andesite (Volcanic).
    - **Felsic:** Granite (Plutonic) / Rhyolite (Volcanic) / Obsidian (Glassy).
* **Sedimentary Petrology & Clastic Diagenesis:**
  - **Weathering & Grain Size:** Gravel ($>2\text{ mm}$) $\to$ Sand ($1/16-2\text{ mm}$) $\to$ Silt ($1/256-1/16\text{ mm}$) $\to$ Clay ($<1/256\text{ mm}$).
  - **Diagenesis & Lithification:** Compaction $\to$ Cementation (Calcite $\text{CaCO}_3$, Silica $\text{SiO}_2$, Hematite $\text{Fe}_2\text{O}_3$) $\to$ Recrystallization $\implies$ Sandstone, Shale, Limestone.
* **Metamorphic Petrology, Barrovian Zones, & P-T Facies (George Barrow 1893, Pentti Eskola 1915):**
  - **Pelitic Barrovian Regional Sequence (Increasing Temperature & Depth):**
    $$\mathbf{\text{Shale (Protolith)} \longrightarrow \text{Slate} \longrightarrow \text{Phyllite} \longrightarrow \text{Schist} \longrightarrow \text{Gneiss} \longrightarrow \text{Migmatite (Partial Melting)}}$$
  - **Barrovian Index Minerals:** Chlorite $\to$ Biotite $\to$ Garnet (Almandine) $\to$ Staurolite $\to$ Kyanite $\to$ Sillimanite.
  - **Metamorphic P-T Facies:**
    - **Zeolite / Greenschist Facies:** Moderate P, moderate T (Chlorite, Actinolite, Epidote).
    - **Amphibolite / Granulite Facies:** High T, medium-high P (Hornblende, Garnet, Pyroxene).
    - **Blueschist Facies:** **High Pressure, Low Temperature (Subduction Zones)**; diagnostic blue **Glaucophane** amphibole and Lawsonite!
    - **Eclogite Facies:** Ultra-high pressure deep mantle subduction ($>1.5\text{ GPa}$); Omphacite pyroxene $+$ Pyrope garnet.

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. The P-T Trajectory Map & Silicate Polymerization Ladder
* As temperature drops in Bowen's series, polymerization increases ($0 \to 1 \to 2 \to 3 \to 4$ shared oxygens).
* Blueschist = Cold slab plunging fast into deep subduction (High P, Low T).
