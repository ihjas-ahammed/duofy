# Key to Flow: Earth and Planetary Sciences

**Subject Area:** `02_Physical_and_Life_Sciences / 06_Earth_and_Planetary_Sciences`

---

## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
Earth & Planetary Sciences analyze the origin, internal structure, surface dynamics, atmospheres, and evolutionary histories of Earth and planetary bodies in the solar system.

---

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. Plate Tectonics & Solid Earth Dynamics
* **Plate Tectonics Unifying Model:** Divergent (Mid-ocean ridges), Convergent (Subduction zones, mountain building), and Transform boundaries (San Andreas fault).
* **Seismology & Earth Interior:** $P$-waves (Compressional, travel through liquid and solid) vs $S$-waves (Shear, travel ONLY through solids $\implies$ proved liquid outer core!).
* **Rock Cycle & Mineralogy:** Igneous (Basalt/Granite), Sedimentary (Limestone/Sandstone), Metamorphic (Gneiss/Marble). Bowen's Reaction Series.

### 2. Atmospheric & Ocean Systems
* **Coriolis Effect:** Deflects moving air/water to the right in Northern Hemisphere, left in Southern Hemisphere.
* **Atmospheric Circulation Cells:** Hadley, Ferrel, and Polar Cells.
* **Thermohaline Circulation:** Global ocean conveyor belt driven by density differences (temperature and salinity).

### 3. Top Recommended Resources
* **Standard Geology Text:** *Earth: An Introduction to Physical Geology* by Tarbuck & Lutgens.
* **Geophysics Classic:** *Geodynamics* by Turcotte & Schubert.
* **Atmospheric Science:** *Atmospheric Science: An Introductory Survey* by Wallace & Hobbs.
