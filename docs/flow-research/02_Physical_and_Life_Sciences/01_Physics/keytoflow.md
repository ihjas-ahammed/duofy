# Key to Flow: Physics (Physical Sciences)

**Subject Area:** `02_Physical_and_Life_Sciences / 01_Physics`

---

## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
Physics is the fundamental science that seeks to understand the behavior of matter, energy, space, and time from quantum scales ($10^{-18}\text{ m}$) to cosmological scales ($10^{26}\text{ m}$).

---

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. The Core Pillar Framework
1. **Classical Mechanics (Lagrangian & Hamiltonian):** Action Principle $\delta S = 0 \implies \frac{d}{dt}\frac{\partial L}{\partial \dot{q}} - \frac{\partial L}{\partial q} = 0$. Poisson Brackets and Canonical Transformations.
2. **Electromagnetism (Maxwell's Equations):**
   * $\nabla \cdot \mathbf{E} = \frac{\rho}{\epsilon_0}$
   * $\nabla \cdot \mathbf{B} = 0$
   * $\nabla \times \mathbf{E} = -\frac{\partial \mathbf{B}}{\partial t}$
   * $\nabla \times \mathbf{B} = \mu_0 \mathbf{J} + \mu_0 \epsilon_0 \frac{\partial \mathbf{E}}{\partial t}$
3. **Quantum Mechanics:** Wavefunction $\psi(x,t)$, Operator formulation, Schrödinger Equation $H\psi = E\psi$, Perturbation Theory.
4. **Thermodynamics & Statistical Physics:** Partition Functions $Z = \sum e^{-\beta E_i}$, Free Energies ($F, G$), Quantum Statistics (Fermi-Dirac & Bose-Einstein).
5. **Special & General Relativity:** Spacetime metric $ds^2 = g_{\mu\nu} dx^\mu dx^\nu$, Geodesic motion, Einstein Field Equations.

### 2. Physical Intuition & Dimensional Analysis
* **Order-of-Magnitude Estimation (Fermi Problems):** Always estimate scale before calculating exact numbers.
* **Buckingham $\pi$ Theorem:** Use dimensional analysis to find scaling laws before solving differential equations.

### 3. Top Recommended Resources
* **Gold Standard Undergrad Series:** *The Feynman Lectures on Physics (Vol 1–3)*.
* **Theoretical Physics Course:** *Landau & Lifshitz Course of Theoretical Physics* (Vols 1–10).
* **Quantum Mechanics Classic:** *Introduction to Quantum Mechanics* by David J. Griffiths.
* **Electrodynamics Classic:** *Introduction to Electrodynamics* by David J. Griffiths.
