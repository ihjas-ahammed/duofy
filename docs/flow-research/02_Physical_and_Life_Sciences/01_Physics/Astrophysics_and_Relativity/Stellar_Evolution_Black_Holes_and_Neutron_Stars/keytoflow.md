## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
**Stellar Evolution & Relativistic Compact Objects** trace the life cycle of stars as an ongoing battle between gravitational collapse and internal thermal/quantum degeneracy pressure:
* **Hydrostatic Equilibrium (Stellar Structure):**
  $$\frac{dP}{dr} = -\frac{G M(r) \rho(r)}{r^2}$$
* **Degeneracy Pressure & Mass Limits:**
  - **White Dwarfs (Electron Degeneracy):** Supported by Pauli exclusion of non-relativistic / relativistic electrons.
    $$\text{Chandrasekhar Mass Limit (1930): } M_{\text{Ch}} \approx 1.44 M_\odot$$
  - **Neutron Stars & Pulsars (Neutron Degeneracy & Strong Nuclear Force):** Formed via core-collapse supernovae (electron capture $p + e^- \to n + \nu_e$).
    $$\text{Tolman-Oppenheimer-Volkoff (TOV) Limit (1939): } M_{\text{TOV}} \approx 2.1 - 2.3 M_\odot$$
  - **Black Holes ($M_{\text{core}} > M_{\text{TOV}}$):** Complete gravitational collapse to a spacetime singularity surrounded by an event horizon.

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. Nuclear Burning Stages & The H-R Diagram
* **Main Sequence:** Hydrogen fusion via **p-p Chain** ($M \lesssim 1.3 M_\odot$, Sun) or **CNO Cycle** ($M \gtrsim 1.3 M_\odot$).
* **Post-Main Sequence:** **Triple-Alpha Process** ($3 \, ^4\text{He} \to \, ^{12}\text{C}$ at $T \sim 10^8\text{ K}$), followed by Carbon, Neon, Oxygen, and Silicon burning shells up to **$^{56}\text{Fe}$ / $^{56}\text{Ni}$** (peak nuclear binding energy per nucleon $\sim 8.8\text{ MeV}$).

### 2. Rotating Black Holes & Black Hole Thermodynamics
* **The Kerr Metric (Roy Kerr, 1963):** Exact spacetime geometry of a rotating, uncharged black hole with spin parameter $a = J/(Mc)$:
  - **Ergosphere:** Region outside the event horizon where spacetime is dragged at $>c$ (Frame Dragging / Lense-Thirring effect).
  - **Penrose Process (1969):** Extracting rotational energy up to $29\%$ of the black hole's mass from the ergosphere.
* **Black Hole Thermodynamics & Hawking Radiation (Stephen Hawking, 1974):**
  - **Hawking Temperature:** $T_H = \frac{\hbar c^3}{8\pi G M k_B}$.
  - **Bekenstein-Hawking Entropy:** $S_{\text{BH}} = \frac{k_B c^3 A}{4 G \hbar} = \frac{k_B A}{4 \ell_P^2}$.
