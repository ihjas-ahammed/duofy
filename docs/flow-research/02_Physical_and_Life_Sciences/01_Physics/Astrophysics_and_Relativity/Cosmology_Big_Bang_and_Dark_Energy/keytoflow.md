## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
**Modern Physical Cosmology** models the universe as a homogeneous and isotropic expanding spacetime described by the **$\Lambda\text{CDM}$ Concordance Model**:
* **The FLRW Metric (Friedmann-Lemaître-Robertson-Walker):**
  $$ds^2 = -c^2 dt^2 + a(t)^2 \left[ \frac{dr^2}{1 - kr^2} + r^2 (d\theta^2 + \sin^2\theta \, d\phi^2) \right]$$
  *(Scale factor $a(t)$ tracks cosmic expansion; curvature $k \in \{-1, 0, +1\}$).*
* **The First & Second Friedmann Equations (Alexander Friedmann, 1922):**
  $$H^2 = \left(\frac{\dot{a}}{a}\right)^2 = \frac{8\pi G}{3}\rho - \frac{kc^2}{a^2} + \frac{\Lambda c^2}{3}$$
  $$\frac{\ddot{a}}{a} = -\frac{4\pi G}{3}\left(\rho + \frac{3p}{c^2}\right) + \frac{\Lambda c^2}{3}$$
* **The Critical Density $\rho_c$ & Density Parameters $\Omega_i$:**
  $$\rho_c = \frac{3H_0^2}{8\pi G} \approx 8.5 \times 10^{-27} \text{ kg/m}^3, \qquad \Omega_m + \Omega_r + \Omega_\Lambda + \Omega_k = 1$$
  *(Current universe: $\Omega_\Lambda \approx 0.69$, $\Omega_{\text{CDM}} \approx 0.26$, $\Omega_b \approx 0.05$, $\Omega_k \approx 0$).*

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. Cosmic Evolution Timeline & The $\Lambda\text{CDM}$ Model
* **1. Cosmic Inflation (Alan Guth, 1981):** Exponential expansion at $t \sim 10^{-36}\text{ s}$ driven by scalar inflaton field $V(\phi)$, solving the Flatness and Horizon problems and seeding quantum density fluctuations.
* **2. Big Bang Nucleosynthesis (BBN, $t \sim 3\text{ min}$):** Fusion of primordial elements ($\sim 75\% \, ^1\text{H}$, $\sim 25\% \, ^4\text{He}$, traces of $\text{D}, ^3\text{He}, ^7\text{Li}$).
* **3. Recombination & CMB ($z \approx 1100, t \sim 380,000\text{ yr}$):** Electrons and protons bind into neutral hydrogen; photons decouple to form the **Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB, $T_0 = 2.7255\text{ K}$)**.
* **4. Dark Energy & Accelerated Expansion ($z < 0.5$):** Dark energy with negative pressure equation of state $w = p/(\rho c^2) \approx -1$ overtakes matter, causing accelerated expansion (Perlmutter, Schmidt, Riess 1998 Type Ia Supernovae).

### 2. CMB Power Spectrum & Acoustic Peaks
* Baryon-photon acoustic oscillations create characteristic peaks in the angular power spectrum $C_\ell$:
  - **1st Peak ($\ell \approx 220$):** Location measures spatial geometry ($\Omega_k = 0$, flat universe).
  - **2nd Peak:** Ratio to 1st peak measures **baryon density $\Omega_b$**.
  - **3rd Peak:** Ratio measures **cold dark matter density $\Omega_{\text{CDM}}$**.
