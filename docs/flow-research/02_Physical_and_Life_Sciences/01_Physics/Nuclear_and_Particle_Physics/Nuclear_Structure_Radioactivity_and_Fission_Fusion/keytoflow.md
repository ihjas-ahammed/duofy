## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
**Nuclear Physics** governs the static structure, quantum shell configurations, radioactive decays, and high-energy transformations of atomic nuclei:
* **The Liquid Drop Model & Semi-Empirical Mass Formula (Bethe-Weizsäcker SEMF, 1935):**
  $$B(A, Z) = a_v A - a_s A^{2/3} - a_c \frac{Z(Z-1)}{A^{1/3}} - a_a \frac{(A - 2Z)^2}{A} + \delta(A, Z)$$
  - Explains the Binding Energy per Nucleon curve ($B/A \approx 8.8\text{ MeV/nucleon}$ peaking at $^{56}\text{Fe}/^{62}\text{Ni}$).
* **The Nuclear Shell Model (Maria Goeppert Mayer & J.H.D. Jensen, 1949):**
  - Explains **Magic Numbers** ($2, 8, 20, 28, 50, 82, 126$) by introducing strong attractive **Spin-Orbit Coupling ($\hat{V}_{SO} = -f(r)\mathbf{L}\cdot\mathbf{S}$)**, splitting $j = \ell + 1/2$ downward and creating large shell gaps.
* **Radioactivity & Decay Modes:**
  - **Decay Law:** $N(t) = N_0 e^{-\lambda t}$, $T_{1/2} = \frac{\ln 2}{\lambda} \approx \frac{0.693}{\lambda}$.
  - **Alpha Decay ($\alpha$):** Quantum mechanical tunneling through the Coulomb barrier (Gamow Theory, Geiger-Nuttall Law).
  - **Beta Decay ($\beta^-, \beta^+, \text{EC}$):** Weak interaction mediated by $W^\pm$ bosons; continuous beta spectrum explained by Pauli's Neutrino hypothesis ($\bar{\nu}_e, \nu_e$).
  - **Gamma Decay ($\gamma$):** Electromagnetic transitions between excited nuclear states.
* **Fission & Fusion Energy:**
  - **Fission:** Induced splitting of actinides ($^{235}\text{U} + n \to \text{fragments} + 2.5 n + 200\text{ MeV}$).
  - **Fusion:** Light isotope coalescence ($^{2}\text{H} + \,^{3}\text{H} \to \,^{4}\text{He} + n + 17.6\text{ MeV}$); ignition governed by the **Lawson Criterion ($n T \tau_E \ge 3 \times 10^{21}\text{ keV}\cdot\text{s/m}^3$)**.

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. Two Complementary Nuclear Models
* **Collective / Liquid Drop Model:** Dominates gross bulk properties, fission barriers, and nuclear vibrations/rotations.
* **Single-Particle Shell Model:** Dominates nuclear spins, parities, magnetic dipole moments, and magic stability.

### 2. Top Recommended Resources
* **The Definitive Standard Textbook:** *Introductory Nuclear Physics* by Kenneth S. Krane (Wiley).
* **Comprehensive Theoretical Guide:** *Introductory Nuclear Physics* by Samuel S.M. Wong (Wiley-VCH).
* **Classical Foundations:** *Nuclear Physics* by Irving Kaplan.
