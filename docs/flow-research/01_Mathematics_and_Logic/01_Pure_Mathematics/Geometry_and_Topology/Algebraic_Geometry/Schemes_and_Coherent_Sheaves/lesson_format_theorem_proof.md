## 🎯 Pedagogical Blueprint & Objective
Guide students through the proof that the global sections functor $\Gamma(\operatorname{Spec}(R), \mathcal{O}_{\operatorname{Spec}(R)}) \cong R$ is an exact equivalence of categories between $R$-modules and quasi-coherent sheaves, and prove **Serre's Affineness Theorem**.

## 🤖 LLM Prompt Generation Rules & Directives

1. **Slide 1 (`theory`):** State the Theorem: For any commutative ring $R$, the global sections of the structure sheaf on $X = \operatorname{Spec}(R)$ satisfy $\Gamma(X, \mathcal{O}_X) \cong R$, and the functor $M \mapsto \widetilde{M}$ is an exact equivalence from $R\text{-Mod}$ to $\mathbf{QCoh}(X)$.
2. **Slide 2 (`ordering`):** Provide 5 steps of the proof: (1) cover $X$ by principal open sets $D(f_i)$ with $\sum a_i f_i = 1$, (2) given compatible local sections $s_i \in R_{f_i} = \mathcal{O}_X(D(f_i))$, write $s_i = a_i / f_i^n$, (3) agreement on overlaps $D(f_i f_j)$ implies $(f_i f_j)^m (a_i f_j^n - a_j f_i^n) = 0$, (4) replace $f_i$ by $f_i^{n+m}$ so that $a_i f_j = a_j f_i$, (5) assemble global element $a = \sum b_i a_i \in R$ and verify its restriction to each $D(f_i)$ equals $s_i$.
3. **Slide 3 (`quiz`):** Test why the quasi-compactness of $\operatorname{Spec}(R)$ allows reducing any open cover to a finite cover $\bigcup_{i=1}^k D(f_i)$.
4. **Slide 4 (`proof`):** Interactive derivation showing higher sheaf cohomology vanishes $H^i(\operatorname{Spec}(R), \widetilde{M}) = 0$ for all $i > 0$.
5. **Slide 5 (`one_word`):** Plain-text recall of the Czech-named cohomology theory using open covers (Cech / Čech).
