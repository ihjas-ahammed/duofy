# Key to Flow: Euclidean and Non-Euclidean Geometry

**Subject Area:** `01_Mathematics_and_Logic / 01_Pure_Mathematics / Geometry_and_Topology / Euclidean_and_Non_Euclidean_Geometry`

---

## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
Geometry explores the structure of space, distance, and curvature. **Non-Euclidean Geometries** arise by modifying Euclid's Parallel Postulate:
* **Hyperbolic Geometry (Lobachevskian):** Infinite parallel lines through a point; triangle angles sum to $< 180^\circ$; constant negative curvature ($K < 0$).
* **Elliptic / Spherical Geometry (Riemannian):** Zero parallel lines through a point; triangle angles sum to $> 180^\circ$; constant positive curvature ($K > 0$).

---

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. The Three Models of Geometry
| Property | Euclidean | Hyperbolic (Poincaré Disk / Half-Plane) | Elliptic / Spherical |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Parallel Lines** | Exactly 1 | Infinitely Many | None (0) |
| **Triangle Angle Sum** | $= 180^\circ$ | $< 180^\circ$ (Defect $\propto$ Area) | $> 180^\circ$ (Excess $\propto$ Area) |
| **Curvature ($K$)** | $0$ | $-1$ | $+1$ |

### 2. Geometric Transformation & Invariance (Klein's Erlangen Program)
Study geometry as the invariant properties under a group of transformations:
* **Euclidean:** Rigid motions (Translations, Rotations, Reflections).
* **Hyperbolic:** Möbius transformations preserving the unit disk $\mathbb{D}$.

### 3. Top Recommended Resources
* **Intuitive & Historical:** *Euclidean and Non-Euclidean Geometries: Development and History* by Marvin Jay Greenberg.
* **Modern & Visual:** *Geometry from a Differentiable Viewpoint* by John McCleary.
