## 🎯 Pedagogical Blueprint & Objective
Introduce the Topology Axioms ($\emptyset, X, \bigcup, \bigcap_{\text{finite}}$), the definition and verification of a Basis $\mathcal{B}$ and Subbasis $\mathcal{S}$, Product vs Box Topologies, and Quotient Topologies for gluing spaces.

## 🤖 LLM Prompt Generation Rules & Directives

1. **Slide 1 (`theory`):** State the axioms for a topology $\mathcal{T} \subseteq \mathcal{P}(X)$, the definition of a basis $\mathcal{B}$ (covering condition and intersection interpolation), and generated topology $\mathcal{T}(\mathcal{B})$.
2. **Slide 2 (`concept_pieces`):** Deconstruct subbasis $\mathcal{S}$ (finite intersections), subspace topology ($U \cap A$), product topology (finite non-trivial coordinates), and quotient topology (gluing via projection $\pi$).
3. **Slide 3 (`matching`):** Pair 4 standard topologies on $\mathbb{R}$ (Standard, Discrete, Indiscrete/Trivial, Lower Limit / Sorgenfrey $\mathbb{R}_\ell$) with their basis elements.
4. **Slide 4 (`fill_in_blank`):** Single sentence blank testing recall that the collection of all finite intersections of elements of a subbasis forms a basis for the topology. Plain text only.
5. **Slide 5 (`quiz`):** Multiple choice question asking why the Product Topology on an infinite product $\prod X_\alpha$ requires basis elements to have $U_\alpha = X_\alpha$ for all but finitely many indices (to make projection maps continuous with the coarsest possible topology).
