**Architectural Rule:** EXACTLY 5 SLIDES PER LESSON. Active Recall & Practice-First Pedagogy.

## 🎯 Pedagogical Blueprint & Objective
Guide students through the proof of the **Basis Verification Theorem** (a collection $\mathcal{B}$ generates a valid topology satisfying all open set axioms $\iff$ $\mathcal{B}$ satisfies the two basis axioms) and prove the **Topology Comparison Criterion** ($\mathcal{T}'$ is finer than $\mathcal{T}$).

## 🤖 LLM Prompt Generation Rules & Directives

1. **Slide 1 (`theory`):** State the Theorem: Let $\mathcal{B}$ be a collection of subsets of $X$. The collection $\mathcal{T}(\mathcal{B}) = \{U \subseteq X \mid \forall x \in U, \exists B \in \mathcal{B} \text{ with } x \in B \subseteq U\}$ is a valid topology on $X$ if and only if $\mathcal{B}$ covers $X$ and satisfies the intersection condition ($x \in B_1 \cap B_2 \implies \exists B_3 \subseteq B_1 \cap B_2$).
2. **Slide 2 (`ordering`):** Provide 5 steps of the proof verifying finite intersections of open sets: (1) let $U_1, U_2 \in \mathcal{T}(\mathcal{B})$ and choose $x \in U_1 \cap U_2$, (2) by definition of $\mathcal{T}(\mathcal{B})$, choose $B_1 \in \mathcal{B}$ with $x \in B_1 \subseteq U_1$ and $B_2 \in \mathcal{B}$ with $x \in B_2 \subseteq U_2$, (3) then $x \in B_1 \cap B_2$, (4) by the second basis axiom, choose $B_3 \in \mathcal{B}$ such that $x \in B_3 \subseteq B_1 \cap B_2 \subseteq U_1 \cap U_2$, (5) since for every $x \in U_1 \cap U_2$ there exists such a $B_3$, $U_1 \cap U_2 \in \mathcal{T}(\mathcal{B})$.
3. **Slide 3 (`quiz`):** Test the Lemma on comparing topologies: $\mathcal{T}'$ generated by $\mathcal{B}'$ is finer than $\mathcal{T}$ generated by $\mathcal{B}$ ($\mathcal{T} \subseteq \mathcal{T}'$) $\iff$ for each $x \in X$ and each $B \in \mathcal{B}$ containing $x$, there is a $B' \in \mathcal{B}'$ such that $x \in B' \subseteq B$.
4. **Slide 4 (`proof`):** Interactive derivation showing that a map $g: Y \to Z$ from a quotient space $Y = X/\sim$ is continuous $\iff$ the composite $g \circ \pi: X \to Z$ is continuous.
5. **Slide 5 (`one_word`):** Plain-text recall of the property of a space having a countable basis for its topology (second-countable).
