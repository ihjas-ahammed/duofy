# Duofy Reusable Lesson Format: Dimension Theory - Theorem & Proof Derivation

**Target Topic:** `01_Mathematics_and_Logic / 01_Pure_Mathematics / Algebra / Commutative_Algebra / Dimension_Theory`  
**Lesson Format Type:** `theorem_proof`  
**Architectural Rule:** EXACTLY 5 SLIDES PER LESSON. Active Recall & Practice-First Pedagogy.

---

## 🎯 Pedagogical Blueprint & Objective
Guide students through fundamental theorems in dimension theory, notably **Krull's Principal Ideal Theorem** (*Hauptidealsatz*), **Krull's Height Theorem**, and **Noether Normalization Lemma**, teaching logical step sequencing and geometric codimension deductions.

---

## 🤖 LLM Prompt Generation Rules & Directives

1. **Slide 1 (`theory`):** State Krull's Principal Ideal Theorem: Let $R$ be a Noetherian ring, $x \in R$ a non-unit. If $\mathfrak{p}$ is a minimal prime over $(x)$, then $\text{ht}(\mathfrak{p}) \le 1$. Double-escape backslashes (`\\mathfrak{p}`, `\\le`).
2. **Slide 2 (`ordering`):** Provide 4 to 5 proof steps demonstrating localization at $\mathfrak{p}$, quotienting by minimal primes, and using Artin-Rees or symbolic powers.
3. **Slide 3 (`quiz`):** Test why localization at $\mathfrak{p}$ reduces the theorem to a local Noetherian ring of dimension $\le 1$.
4. **Slide 4 (`proof`):** Step-by-step interactive derivation of the generalization to an ideal generated by $n$ elements having $\text{ht}(\mathfrak{p}) \le n$.
5. **Slide 5 (`one_word`):** Plain-text recall of the name of Krull's theorem or Noetherian property. **STRICT RULE (Rule 11):** Plain text, NO LaTeX `$`.
