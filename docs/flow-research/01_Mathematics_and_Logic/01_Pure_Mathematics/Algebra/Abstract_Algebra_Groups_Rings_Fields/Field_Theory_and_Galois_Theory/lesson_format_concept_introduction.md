# Duofy Reusable Lesson Format: Field & Galois Theory - Concept Introduction

**Target Topic:** `01_Mathematics_and_Logic / 01_Pure_Mathematics / Algebra / Abstract_Algebra_Groups_Rings_Fields / Field_Theory_and_Galois_Theory`  
**Lesson Format Type:** `concept_introduction`  
**Architectural Rule:** EXACTLY 5 SLIDES PER LESSON. Active Recall & Practice-First Pedagogy.

---

## 🎯 Pedagogical Blueprint & Objective
Introduce Field Extensions ($K/F$), Splitting Fields, Normal/Separable Extensions, and the Galois Group ($\text{Gal}(K/F)$) using dual lattice mental models (Intermediate Fields $\iff$ Subgroups).
