**Target Topic:** `01_Mathematics_and_Logic / 05_Mathematical_Logic_and_Foundations / Category_Theory`

## 🎯 Pedagogical Blueprint & Objective
Train students to verify Free-Forgetful Adjunctions ($F \\dashv U$), construct commutative pullback/pushout diagrams, and evaluate Yoneda natural transformations.
