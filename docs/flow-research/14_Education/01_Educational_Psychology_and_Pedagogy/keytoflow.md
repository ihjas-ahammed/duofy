# Key to Flow: Educational Psychology and Pedagogy

**Subject Area:** `14_Education / 01_Educational_Psychology_and_Pedagogy`

---

## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
Educational Psychology and Pedagogy study human learning processes, instructional design, cognitive development, motivation, and active teaching methodologies.

---

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. Key Learning Theories & Pedagogical Models
* **Constructivism (Piaget, Vygotsky):** Learners construct knowledge actively. Vygotsky's **Zone of Proximal Development (ZPD)** and Scaffolding.
* **Cognitive Load Theory (Sweller):** Managing Intrinsic, Extraneous, and Germane cognitive load.
* **Bloom's Taxonomy (Revised):** Remember $\to$ Understand $\to$ Apply $\to$ Analyze $\to$ Evaluate $\to$ Create.
* **Pedagogical Strategies:** Flipped Classroom, Problem-Based Learning (PBL), Inquiry-Based Learning.

### 2. Top Recommended Resources
* **Educational Psychology Bible:** *Educational Psychology* by Anita Woolfolk.
* **Instructional Design Standard:** *The Systematic Design of Instruction* by Dick, Carey, and Carey.
