# Key to Flow: Educational Leadership and Policy

**Subject Area:** `14_Education / 03_Educational_Leadership_and_Policy`

---

## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
Educational Leadership and Policy examine the administration, governance, institutional management, equity policy, and organizational change within primary, secondary, and higher education systems.

---

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. Leadership Models & School Policy
* **Instructional Leadership:** Focusing principal/administrator action on improving teacher instruction and student learning outcomes.
* **Transformational Leadership in Education:** Inspiring school culture, shared vision, professional learning communities (PLCs).
* **Education Policy Reform:** School choice (Vouchers, Charter schools), Standardized testing policy, Educational equity & funding formulas.

### 2. Top Recommended Resources
* **Educational Leadership Classic:** *Educational Leadership: Concepts and Practices* by Fred C. Lunenburg & Allan C. Ornstein.
* **School Reform Standard:** *The New Meaning of Educational Change* by Michael Fullan.
