# Key to Flow: Educational Technology and E-Learning

**Subject Area:** `14_Education / 05_Educational_Technology_and_E_Learning`

---

## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
Educational Technology (EdTech) and E-Learning integrate digital tools, learning management systems (LMS), artificial intelligence, and instructional media to enhance teaching effectiveness and remote learning accessibility.

---

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. EdTech Frameworks & Architecture
* **SAMR Model:** Substitution $\to$ Augmentation $\to$ Modification $\to$ Redefinition (Evaluating technology integration level).
* **TPACK Framework:** Integration of **T**echnological, **P**edagogical, and **C**ontent **K**nowledge.
* **ADDIE Model (Instructional Design):** Analysis, Design, Development, Implementation, Evaluation.
* **Digital Learning Platforms:** Learning Management Systems (Canvas, Moodle), SCORM standards, Learning Analytics, AI Tutors.

### 2. Top Recommended Resources
* **EdTech Standard:** *Trends and Issues in Instructional Design and Technology* by Robert A. Reiser & John V. Dempsey.
* **TPACK Classic:** *Handbook of Technological Pedagogical Content Knowledge (TPACK) for Educators*.
