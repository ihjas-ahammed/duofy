# Key to Flow: Curriculum Design and Assessment

**Subject Area:** `14_Education / 02_Curriculum_Design_and_Assessment`

---

## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
Curriculum Design and Assessment systematically develop educational programs, course syllabi, learning outcomes, and evaluation instruments to measure student learning validly and reliably.

---

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. Curriculum Architecture & Assessment Types
* **Backward Design Model (Wiggins & McTighe - UbD):**
  1. Identify desired results (Learning outcomes).
  2. Determine acceptable evidence (Assessment tasks).
  3. Plan learning experiences and instruction.
* **Assessment Taxonomy:**
  * **Formative Assessment:** Ongoing feedback *for* learning (Quizzes, Exit tickets).
  * **Summative Assessment:** Final evaluation *of* learning (Midterms, Final exams, Standardized testing).
  * **Diagnostic & Diagnostic Assessment:** Pre-assessments.
* **Assessment Quality:** Validity (Construct, Content, Criterion), Reliability (Internal consistency, Inter-rater), Item Response Theory (IRT).

### 2. Top Recommended Resources
* **Backward Design Classic:** *Understanding by Design* by Grant Wiggins & Jay McTighe.
* **Assessment Standard:** *Classroom Assessment: What Teachers Need to Know* by W. James Popham.
