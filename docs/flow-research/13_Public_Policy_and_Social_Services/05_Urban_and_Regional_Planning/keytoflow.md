# Key to Flow: Urban and Regional Planning

**Subject Area:** `13_Public_Policy_and_Social_Services / 05_Urban_and_Regional_Planning`

---

## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
Urban and Regional Planning optimizes land use, infrastructure systems, public transportation, housing, and environmental quality to build sustainable, resilient, and equitable cities.

---

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. Planning Principles & Tools
* **Land Use Planning:** Zoning regulations (Euclidean zoning, Form-based codes, Mixed-use zoning), Master planning, Floor Area Ratio (FAR).
* **Transportation & Smart Cities:** Transit-Oriented Development (TOD), Bus Rapid Transit (BRT), Micro-mobility, Smart transit sensors.
* **Urban Sustainability:** Green infrastructure, Urban heat island mitigation, Climate adaptation master plans.

### 2. Top Recommended Resources
* **Urban Planning Classic:** *Urban Land Use Planning* by Philip R. Berke et al.
* **Planning Theory Standard:** *Readings in Planning Theory* by Susan S. Fainstein & James DeFilippis.
* **Cities Classic:** *The Image of the City* by Kevin Lynch.
