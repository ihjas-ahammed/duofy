# Key to Flow: Social Work and Community Development

**Subject Area:** `13_Public_Policy_and_Social_Services / 04_Social_Work_and_Community_Development`

---

## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
Social Work and Community Development empower individuals, families, vulnerable groups, and communities to enhance social functioning, combat systemic disadvantage, and advocate for human rights.

---

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. Social Work Levels & Methods
* **Micro Social Work:** Clinical counseling, case management, individual therapy.
* **Mezzo Social Work:** Group work, family intervention, local community organizations.
* **Macro Social Work:** Community development, policy advocacy, systemic social reform.
* **Core Principles:** Person-in-Environment (PIE) perspective, Anti-oppressive practice, Trauma-informed care.

### 2. Top Recommended Resources
* **Social Work Classic:** *Introduction to Social Work* by Lisa E. Cox et al.
* **Community Development Standard:** *Community Development: A Critical Approach* by Margaret Ledwith.
