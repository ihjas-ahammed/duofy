# Key to Flow: Public Policy Analysis

**Subject Area:** `13_Public_Policy_and_Social_Services / 03_Public_Policy_Analysis`

---

## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
Public Policy Analysis uses quantitative (Cost-Benefit Analysis, Randomized Control Trials) and qualitative tools to evaluate public interventions, predict policy outcomes, and design effective social and economic interventions.

---

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. The Policy Cycle & Evaluation Framework
* **The Policy Cycle:** Problem Identification $\to$ Agenda Setting $\to$ Policy Formulation $\to$ Decision Making $\to$ Implementation $\to$ Evaluation.
* **Bardach's Eightfold Path for Policy Analysis:**
  1. Define the problem.
  2. Assemble evidence.
  3. Construct alternatives.
  4. Select criteria (Efficiency, Equity, Political Feasibility).
  5. Project outcomes.
  6. Confront trade-offs.
  7. Decide!
  8. Tell your story.
* **Evaluation Tools:** Cost-Benefit Analysis (CBA / Net Present Value NPV), Cost-Effectiveness Analysis (CEA), Randomized Control Trials (RCTs / Duflo-Banerjee-Kremer experimental methodology).

### 2. Top Recommended Resources
* **Policy Analysis Classic:** *A Practical Guide for Policy Analysis: The Eightfold Path to More Effective Problem Solving* by Eugene Bardach & Eric M. Patashnik.
* **Impact Evaluation Bible:** *Impact Evaluation in Practice* by Paul J. Gertler et al. (World Bank Free PDF!).
