# Key to Flow: Public Administration and Governance

**Subject Area:** `13_Public_Policy_and_Social_Services / 01_Public_Administration_and_Governance`

---

## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
Public Administration examines how public sector institutions, civil service bureaucracies, and government agencies implement public policies and deliver public goods efficiently, equitably, and ethically.

---

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. The Governance Paradigm Evolution
* **Weberian Ideal Bureaucracy:** Hierarchy, specialization, impersonal rules, merit-based civil service.
* **New Public Management (NPM):** Introducing market mechanisms, performance metrics, privatization, and customer orientation to the public sector.
* **Good Governance & E-Governance:** Transparency, Accountability, Public Participation, Digital service delivery (GovTech).

### 2. Top Recommended Resources
* **Public Admin Classic:** *Public Administration: Concepts and Cases* by Richard J. Stillman II.
* **Governance Standard:** *Governance: An Introduction to Public Administration* by B. Guy Peters.
