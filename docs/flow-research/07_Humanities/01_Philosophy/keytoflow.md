# Key to Flow: Philosophy

**Subject Area:** `07_Humanities / 01_Philosophy`

---

## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
Philosophy is the systematic study of fundamental questions concerning existence, knowledge, values, reason, mind, and language.

---

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. The Core Philosophical Branches
* **Epistemology:** What is knowledge? Justified True Belief (JTB) & Gettier Problems, Rationalism vs Empiricism, Skepticism.
* **Metaphysics:** Mind-Body Problem (Dualism vs Physicalism), Free Will vs Determinism, Ontology (What exists?).
* **Ethics:**
  * **Normative:** Deontology (Kant's Categorical Imperative), Consequentialism/Utilitarianism (Bentham, Mill), Virtue Ethics (Aristotle).
  * **Meta-ethics & Applied Ethics:** Bioethics, AI Ethics.
* **Logic:** Syllogistic logic, Propositional & Predicate calculus, Fallacy detection.

### 2. Top Recommended Resources
* **Introductory Classic:** *The Problems of Philosophy* by Bertrand Russell.
* **History of Philosophy:** *A History of Western Philosophy* by Bertrand Russell.
* **Ethics Standard:** *Justice: What's the Right Thing to Do?* by Michael J. Sandel.
* **Online Reference:** Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy (SEP - The gold standard philosophy database!).

---

## 🎯 Problem-Solving Checklist
- [ ] Can you reconstruct philosophical arguments in standard form (Premises $\to$ Conclusion)?
- [ ] Can you analyze Gettier counterexamples to the Justified True Belief definition of knowledge?
- [ ] Can you compare Deontological vs Utilitarian outcomes in moral dilemmas (e.g. Trolley Problem)?
