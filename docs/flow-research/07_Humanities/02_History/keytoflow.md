# Key to Flow: History

**Subject Area:** `07_Humanities / 02_History`

---

## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
History examines past human events, societies, cultures, and institutions using critical analysis of primary sources, archival evidence, and historiographical frameworks.

---

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. Historical Analysis Framework
* **Primary vs Secondary Sources:** Evaluating bias, provenance, audience, and context of historical artifacts/documents.
* **Historiography:** Analyzing how historical interpretations change over time (e.g., Marxist history, Annales school, Subaltern studies, Post-colonialism).
* **Major Historical Epochs:**
  * **Ancient:** Mesopotamian, Egyptian, Indus Valley, Greco-Roman, Han Dynasty.
  * **Medieval:** Feudal Europe, Islamic Golden Age, Tang/Song Dynasties, Crusades.
  * **Modern:** Renaissance, Enlightenment, Industrial Revolution, Imperialism, World Wars, Cold War, Globalization.

### 2. Top Recommended Resources
* **Historiography Classic:** *What is History?* by E.H. Carr.
* **Global History Classic:** *Guns, Germs, and Steel* by Jared Diamond.
* **World History Text:** *A History of the Modern World* by Palmer, Colton, Kramer.

---

## 🎯 Problem-Solving Checklist
- [ ] Can you evaluate primary source documents for bias, context, and reliability?
- [ ] Can you compare differing historiographical interpretations of major events (e.g. Causes of WWI)?
- [ ] Can you analyze the social, economic, and technological drivers of the Industrial Revolution?
