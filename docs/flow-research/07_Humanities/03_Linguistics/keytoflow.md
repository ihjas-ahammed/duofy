# Key to Flow: Linguistics

**Subject Area:** `07_Humanities / 03_Linguistics`

---

## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
Linguistics is the scientific study of language structure, sound systems, word formation, sentence syntax, meaning, and socio-cultural language usage.

---

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. Structural Linguistics Levels
* **Phonetics & Phonology:** International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA), Articulatory features (Voicing, Place, Manner), Phonemes vs Allophones.
* **Morphology:** Morphemes (Free vs Bound), Affixation, Inflection vs Derivation.
* **Syntax:** Generative Grammar (Noam Chomsky), Phrase Structure Trees ($S \to NP + VP$), X-Bar Theory.
* **Semantics & Pragmatics:** Truth conditions, Entailment, Speech Acts (Austin/Searle), Gricean Maxims of Conversation.
* **Sociolinguistics & Computational:** Dialects, Language Change, Natural Language Processing.

### 2. Top Recommended Resources
* **Linguistics Bible:** *An Introduction to Language* by Victoria Fromkin, Robert Rodman, Nina Hyams.
* **Syntax Classic:** *Syntax: A Generative Introduction* by Andrew Carnie.
* **Phonetics Classic:** *A Course in Phonetics* by Peter Ladefoged & Keith Johnson.

---

## 🎯 Problem-Solving Checklist
- [ ] Can you transcribe spoken English into International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA)?
- [ ] Can you draw syntax trees ($NP, VP, PP, CP$) for complex sentences?
- [ ] Can you analyze morphological data from unfamiliar languages to isolate morphemes?
- [ ] Can you apply Gricean Maxims (Quantity, Quality, Relation, Manner) to detect conversational implicatures?
