# Key to Flow: Organizational Behavior and Human Resource Management

**Subject Area:** `11_Business_and_Commerce / 05_Organizational_Behavior_and_HRM`

---

## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
Organizational Behavior (OB) and Human Resource Management (HRM) analyze individual, group, and organizational dynamics to optimize workforce performance, leadership, motivation, culture, and talent management.

---

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. OB & HRM Frameworks
* **Motivation Theories:** Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs, Herzberg's Two-Factor Theory (Hygiene vs Motivators), Vroom's Expectancy Theory ($E \to P \to O$).
* **Leadership Models:** Transformational vs Transactional Leadership, Situational Leadership (Hersey-Blanchard).
* **HR Lifecycle:** Talent Acquisition, Onboarding, Performance Appraisal (360-Degree Feedback, OKRs), Compensation & Benefits, Labor Relations.

### 2. Top Recommended Resources
* **Organizational Behavior Classic:** *Organizational Behavior* by Stephen P. Robbins & Timothy A. Judge.
* **Human Resource Management Standard:** *Human Resource Management* by Gary Dessler.
