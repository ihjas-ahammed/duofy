# Key to Flow: Operations, Supply Chain, and Analytics

**Subject Area:** `11_Business_and_Commerce / 06_Operations_Supply_Chain_and_Analytics`

---

## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
Operations & Supply Chain Management oversees the transformation of inputs into finished goods/services, optimizing inventory, logistics, quality control, and data-driven process efficiency.

---

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. Operations Core Toolkit
* **Inventory Management:** Economic Order Quantity ($\text{EOQ} = \sqrt{\frac{2 D S}{H}}$), Just-In-Time (JIT), Safety Stock.
* **Process Analysis:** Little's Law ($\text{Work in Process (WIP)} = \text{Throughput Rate (R)} \times \text{Flow Time (T)}$), Bottleneck analysis, Capacity Utilization.
* **Quality Management:** Total Quality Management (TQM), Six Sigma ($DPMO < 3.4$), DMAIC process, Control Charts ($X$-bar and $R$ charts).
* **Supply Chain Management:** Bullwhip Effect, Logistics, Warehousing, Supply Chain Analytics.

### 2. Top Recommended Resources
* **Operations Classic:** *Operations Management* by Jay Heizer, Barry Render, Chuck Munson.
* **Supply Chain Bible:** *Supply Chain Management: Strategy, Planning, and Operation* by Sunil Chopra.
* **Process Analysis Classic:** *Matching Supply with Demand* by Gerard Cachon & Christian Terwiesch.
