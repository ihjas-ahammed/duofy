# Key to Flow: Marketing and Consumer Research

**Subject Area:** `11_Business_and_Commerce / 04_Marketing_and_Consumer_Research`

---

## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
Marketing creates, communicates, delivers, and exchanges offerings that have value for customers, clients, partners, and society at large.

---

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. The STP & 4 Ps Marketing Framework
* **STP Process:**
  * **Segmentation:** Demographic, Geographic, Psychographic, Behavioral.
  * **Targeting:** Evaluating segment attractiveness & fit.
  * **Positioning:** Brand positioning statement and perceptual mapping.
* **The 4 Ps (Marketing Mix):** Product, Price (Value-based, Cost-plus, Skimming, Penetration), Place (Distribution channels), Promotion (Integrated Marketing Communications - IMC).
* **Digital & Performance Marketing:** Customer Lifetime Value (CLV), Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), Funnel Analysis (AIDA: Attention, Interest, Desire, Action), SEO/SEM, Social Media Analytics.

### 2. Top Recommended Resources
* **The Marketing Bible:** *Marketing Management* by Philip Kotler & Kevin Lane Keller.
* **Consumer Behavior Classic:** *Consumer Behavior* by Leon Schiffman & Joseph Wisenblit.
* **Positioning Classic:** *Positioning: The Battle for Your Mind* by Al Ries & Jack Trout.
