# Key to Flow: Finance and Investment

**Subject Area:** `11_Business_and_Commerce / 03_Finance_and_Investment`

---

## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
Finance studies capital allocation under risk and uncertainty, evaluating corporate investment decisions, asset pricing, capital structure, and financial derivatives.

---

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. Valuation & Asset Pricing Architecture
* **Discounted Cash Flow (DCF):** Enterprise Value $\text{EV} = \sum \frac{\text{FCFF}_t}{(1 + \text{WACC})^t} + \frac{\text{Terminal Value}}{(1 + \text{WACC})^N}$.
* **Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC):**
  $$\text{WACC} = \left(\frac{E}{V}\right) r_e + \left(\frac{D}{V}\right) r_d (1 - T)$$
* **Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM):** $r_e = r_f + \beta (E[R_m] - r_f)$.
* **Options & Derivatives:** Black-Scholes-Merton Option Pricing Formula, Binomial Option Tree, Call/Put Options, Futures, Swaps.

### 2. Top Recommended Resources
* **Corporate Finance Bible ("BMA"):** *Principles of Corporate Finance* by Brealey, Myers, Allen.
* **Valuation Classic:** *Damodaran on Valuation* by Aswath Damodaran (The Dean of Valuation!).
* **Derivatives Bible:** *Options, Futures, and Other Derivatives* by John C. Hull.
* **Investment Classic:** *Investments* by Bodie, Kane, Marcus.
