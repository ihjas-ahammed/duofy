# Key to Flow: Business Administration and Strategy

**Subject Area:** `11_Business_and_Commerce / 01_Business_Administration_and_Strategy`

---

## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
Strategic Management aligns firm resources, capabilities, and competitive advantages to achieve long-term profitability, market leadership, and sustainable growth.

---

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. The Strategic Analysis Framework
* **External Analysis:**
  * **Porter's Five Forces:** Threat of New Entrants, Bargaining Power of Buyers, Bargaining Power of Suppliers, Threat of Substitutes, Industry Rivalry.
  * **PESTEL Analysis:** Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Environmental, Legal factors.
* **Internal Analysis:**
  * **Resource-Based View (RBV) & VRIO Framework:** Is a resource **V**aluable, **R**are, **I**nimitable, **O**rganized?
  * **Value Chain Analysis:** Primary activities (Inbound logistics, Operations, Outbound, Marketing, Service) + Support activities.
* **Corporate Strategy:** Ansoff Matrix (Market Penetration, Product Development, Market Development, Diversification), BCG Matrix (Stars, Cash Cows, Question Marks, Dogs).

### 2. Top Recommended Resources
* **Strategy Bible:** *Competitive Strategy: Techniques for Analyzing Industries and Competitors* by Michael E. Porter.
* **Strategic Management Standard:** *Strategic Management: Concepts and Cases* by Fred R. David.
* **Modern Strategy Classic:** *Good Strategy Bad Strategy* by Richard Rumelt.
