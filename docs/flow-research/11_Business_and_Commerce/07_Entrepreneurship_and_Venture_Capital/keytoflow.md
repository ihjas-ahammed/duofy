# Key to Flow: Entrepreneurship and Venture Capital

**Subject Area:** `11_Business_and_Commerce / 07_Entrepreneurship_and_Venture_Capital`

---

## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
Entrepreneurship & Venture Capital focus on identifying market opportunities, building scalable startup business models (Lean Startup), fundraising from Seed to Series A/B/C, and managing venture investment portfolios.

---

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. The Startup & VC Lifecycle
* **The Lean Startup Methodology (Eric Ries):** Build-Measure-Learn feedback loop, Minimum Viable Product (MVP), Pivot vs Persevere.
* **Business Model Design:** Alexander Osterwalder's Business Model Canvas (Value Proposition, Customer Segments, Channels, Revenue Streams, Cost Structure).
* **Venture Capital Deal Mechanics:** Term Sheets, Pre-Money vs Post-Money Valuation ($\text{Post} = \text{Pre} + \text{Investment}$), Cap Tables, Liquidation Preferences, SAFE (Simple Agreement for Future Equity).

### 2. Top Recommended Resources
* **The Lean Startup Classic:** *The Lean Startup* by Eric Ries.
* **Venture Capital Classic:** *Venture Deals: Be Smarter Than Your Lawyer and Venture Capitalist* by Brad Feld & Jason Mendelson.
* **Zero to One Classic:** *Zero to One: Notes on Startups, or How to Build the Future* by Peter Thiel.
