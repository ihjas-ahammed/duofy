# Key to Flow: Accounting and Financial Reporting

**Subject Area:** `11_Business_and_Commerce / 02_Accounting_and_Financial_Reporting`

---

## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
Accounting tracks, measures, and communicates financial information about economic entities under double-entry bookkeeping rules and standard accounting frameworks (GAAP / IFRS).

---

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. The Accounting Equation & Financial Statement Triad
$$\text{Assets} = \text{Liabilities} + \text{Shareholders' Equity}$$
* **The 3 Core Financial Statements:**
  1. **Balance Sheet:** Snapshot of financial position ($\text{Assets} = \text{Liabilities} + \text{Equity}$) at a specific point in time.
  2. **Income Statement:** Performance over time ($\text{Revenue} - \text{Expenses} = \text{Net Income}$). Accrual basis.
  3. **Cash Flow Statement:** Actual cash movements ($\text{CFO} + \text{CFI} + \text{CFF} = \Delta \text{Cash}$).
* **Cost & Management Accounting:** Marginal Costing, Break-Even Analysis ($\text{BEP} = \frac{\text{Fixed Costs}}{\text{Contribution Margin}}$), Standard Costing & Variance Analysis.

### 2. Top Recommended Resources
* **Financial Accounting Classic:** *Financial Accounting* by Libby, Libby, and Hodge.
* **Cost Accounting Standard:** *Cost Accounting: A Managerial Emphasis* by Horngren, Datar, Rajan.
* **Financial Statement Analysis:** *Financial Statement Analysis and Security Valuation* by Stephen Penman.
