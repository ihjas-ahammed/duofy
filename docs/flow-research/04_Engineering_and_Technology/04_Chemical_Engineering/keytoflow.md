# Key to Flow: Chemical Engineering

**Subject Area:** `04_Engineering_and_Technology / 04_Chemical_Engineering`

---

## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
Chemical Engineering converts raw materials into valuable chemical products through physical, chemical, and biological unit operations and reactor processes.

---

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. Transport Phenomena & Unit Operations
* **Mass & Energy Balances:** Input = Output + Accumulation - Generation + Consumption.
* **Transport Phenomena:**
  * Momentum Transport: Fourier's Law / Navier-Stokes.
  * Heat Transport: Conduction, Convection, Heat Exchangers ($Q = U A \Delta T_{\text{LMTD}}$).
  * Mass Transport: Fick's Law of Diffusion ($J_A = -D_{AB} \frac{d C_A}{d z}$).
* **Separation Operations:** Distillation (McCabe-Thiele method for theoretical stages), Liquid-Liquid Extraction, Absorption, Drying.
* **Chemical Reaction Engineering (CRE):**
  * Batch Reactor, Continuous Stirred Tank Reactor (CSTR), Plug Flow Reactor (PFR).
  * Design equations: $V_{\text{PFR}} = F_{A0} \int \frac{d X}{-r_A}$.

### 2. Top Recommended Resources
* **The Chemical Engineering Bible:** *Perry's Chemical Engineers' Handbook*.
* **Transport Phenomena Classic:** *Transport Phenomena* by Bird, Stewart, and Lightfoot (BSL).
* **Reaction Engineering Classic:** *Elements of Chemical Reaction Engineering* by H. Scott Fogler.
