# Key to Flow: Private and Civil Law

**Subject Area:** `10_Law_and_Legal_Studies / 04_Private_and_Civil_Law`

---

## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
Private and Civil Law regulates disputes between private individuals, business entities, and organizations, encompassing Contracts, Torts, Property, and Family Law.

---

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. The Civil Law Pillars
* **Law of Contracts:**
  * **Formation:** Offer, Acceptance, Consideration, Intention to create legal relations.
  * **Vitiating Factors:** Misrepresentation, Mistake, Duress, Undue Influence.
  * **Breach & Remedies:** Expectation Damages, Reliance Damages, Specific Performance, Injunctions.
* **Law of Torts:**
  * **Negligence:** Duty of Care (Donoghue v Stevenson / Caparo test), Breach of Duty (Reasonable person standard), Causation & Remoteness (Wagon Mound).
  * **Other Torts:** Nuisance, Defamation (Libel/Slander), Strict Liability (Rylands v Fletcher), Vicarious Liability.
* **Property Law:** Real Property (Land, Easements, Mortgages) vs Personal Property, Title transfer, Adverse Possession.

### 2. Top Recommended Resources
* **Contracts Classic:** *Farnsworth on Contracts* by E. Allan Farnsworth.
* **Torts Classic:** *Prosser and Keeton on Torts*.
* **UK Law of Contract:** *Treitel on the Law of Contract* by Edwin Peel.
