# Key to Flow: Public and Constitutional Law

**Subject Area:** `10_Law_and_Legal_Studies / 02_Public_and_Constitutional_Law`

---

## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
Public and Constitutional Law governs the structure of state governance, the relationship between government branches (Separation of Powers, Rule of Law), fundamental human rights, administrative accountability, and international law.

---

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. Constitutional & Public Law Architecture
* **Constitutional Principles:** Separation of Powers, Judicial Review (Marbury v. Madison / Basic Structure Doctrine), Rule of Law (Dicey).
* **Fundamental Rights:** Freedom of Speech, Due Process, Equal Protection / Non-discrimination, Right to Privacy.
* **Administrative Law:** Judicial review of executive actions (Ultra Vires doctrine, Natural Justice principles: *Audi Alteram Partem* & *Nemo Judex in Causa Sua*).
* **Public International Law:** Sources of International Law (Article 38(1) ICJ Statute: Treaties, Customary International Law, General Principles), State Sovereignty, UN Charter, Geneva Conventions.

### 2. Top Recommended Resources
* **Constitutional Law Classic:** *Constitutional Law* by Erwin Chemerinsky.
* **Administrative Law Standard:** *Administrative Law* by William Wade & Christopher Forsyth.
* **Public International Law Bible:** *Brownlie's Principles of Public International Law* by Crawford.
