# Key to Flow: Jurisprudence and Legal Theory

**Subject Area:** `10_Law_and_Legal_Studies / 01_Jurisprudence_and_Legal_Theory`

---

## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
Jurisprudence explores the theoretical philosophy of law, asking *What is Law?*, examining legal systems, rights, duties, justice, and authority.

---

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. Major Schools of Legal Thought
* **Natural Law Theory (Aquinas, Fuller, Finnis):** Law is intrinsically tied to morality ("An unjust law is no law at all").
* **Legal Positivism (Austin, Hart, Kelsen):** Law is a set of rules posited by human authority, separate from morality.
  * **John Austin:** Sovereign Command backed by Threat of Sanctions.
  * **H.L.A. Hart:** System of Primary Rules (conduct) & Secondary Rules (recognition, change, adjudication).
  * **Hans Kelsen:** Pure Theory of Law & Basic Norm (*Grundnorm*).
* **Legal Realism (Holmes, Llewellyn):** "The life of the law has not been logic; it has been experience." Focuses on judge-made decisions in practice.
* **Critical Legal Studies (CLS):** Law as an instrument of social power and hierarchy.

### 2. Top Recommended Resources
* **Jurisprudence Classic:** *The Concept of Law* by H.L.A. Hart.
* **Legal Theory Standard:** *Jurisprudence* by Raymond Wacks.
* **Natural Law Classic:** *Natural Law and Natural Rights* by John Finnis.
