# Key to Flow: Corporate, Commercial, and Taxation Law

**Subject Area:** `10_Law_and_Legal_Studies / 05_Corporate_Commercial_and_Taxation_Law`

---

## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
Corporate, Commercial, and Taxation Law governs business entity formation, corporate governance, commercial transactions, mergers and acquisitions, and direct/indirect tax liabilities.

---

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. Corporate & Tax Architecture
* **Corporate Law:** Separate Legal Personality (Salomon v Salomon & Piercing the Corporate Veil), Fiduciary Duties of Directors (Duty of Care & Loyalty, Business Judgment Rule), Shareholder Rights & Minority Protection.
* **Commercial Law:** Sale of Goods, Negotiable Instruments, Secured Transactions (Art 9 UCC), Bankruptcy & Insolvency.
* **Taxation Law:**
  * **Direct Tax:** Income Tax, Capital Gains Tax, Corporate Tax, Transfer Pricing.
  * **Indirect Tax:** Goods and Services Tax (GST) / Value Added Tax (VAT), Customs Duties.

### 2. Top Recommended Resources
* **Corporate Law Bible:** *Corporations and Other Business Organizations* by Eisenberg & Cox.
* **UK Company Law:** *Gower's Principles of Modern Company Law* by Paul Davies.
* **Taxation Law:** *Federal Income Taxation* by Freeland et al.
