# Key to Flow: Criminal Law and Procedure

**Subject Area:** `10_Law_and_Legal_Studies / 03_Criminal_Law_and_Procedure`

---

## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
Criminal Law defines offenses against the public state, requiring proof beyond a reasonable doubt of both a physical criminal act (**Actus Reus**) and a culpable mental state (**Mens Rea**).

---

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. Elements of Crime & Defense Taxonomy
* **Core Criminal Elements:**
  * **Actus Reus:** Voluntary physical act or failure to act when under legal duty.
  * **Mens Rea:** Intention, Knowledge, Recklessness, Negligence, Strict Liability.
  * **Causation:** Factual Causation ("But for" test) and Legal Causation (Proximate cause, intervening acts *novus actus interveniens*).
* **Offenses:** Homicide (Murder vs Manslaughter), Assault, Battery, Theft, Robbery, Fraud, Cybercrime.
* **Defenses:** Self-Defense, Necessity, Duress, Insanity (M'Naghten Rules), Intoxication.
* **Criminal Procedure & Evidence:** Search and Seizure, Exclusionary Rule, Miranda Rights, Hearsay evidence, Burden of Proof.

### 2. Top Recommended Resources
* **Criminal Law Classic:** *Criminal Law* by Wayne R. LaFave.
* **UK Criminal Law Bible:** *Smith, Hogan, and Ormerod's Criminal Law*.
* **Evidence Law Classic:** *McCormick on Evidence*.
