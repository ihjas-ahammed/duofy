# Key to Flow: Clinical Medicine and Subspecialties

**Subject Area:** `05_Medicine_and_Health_Sciences / 03_Clinical_Medicine_and_Subspecialties`

---

## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
Clinical Medicine diagnoses, treats, and prevents non-surgical human diseases using history taking, physical examination, diagnostic investigations, and evidence-based pharmacotherapy across internal medicine and subspecialties.

---

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. Clinical Diagnostic Workflow
$$\text{History Taking} \to \text{Physical Exam} \to \text{Differential Diagnosis} \to \text{Lab/Imaging Investigations} \to \text{Definitive Management}$$

### 2. Major Medical Subspecialties
* **Cardiology:** Ischemic Heart Disease (STEMI/NSTEMI), Heart Failure, Arrhythmias, ECG Interpretation (ST elevation, AV blocks).
* **Pulmonology:** Asthma, COPD, Pneumonia, Tuberculosis, Pulmonary Embolism (Wells Score).
* **Gastroenterology & Hepatology:** Peptic Ulcer Disease, Inflammatory Bowel Disease (Crohn's vs Ulcerative Colitis), Cirrhosis & Portal Hypertension.
* **Nephrology:** Acute Kidney Injury (AKI: Prerenal, Intrinsic, Postrenal), Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD staging), Glomerulonephritis.
* **Endocrinology:** Diabetes Mellitus (HbA1c targets, Insulin regimens), Thyroid disorders (Graves' vs Hashimoto's), Cushing's Syndrome.
* **Neurology:** Ischemic/Hemorrhagic Stroke, Epilepsy, Parkinson's Disease, Multiple Sclerosis.
* **Pediatrics:** Growth milestones, Neonatal Jaundice, APGAR score, Pediatric immunization.
* **Psychiatry:** Major Depressive Disorder, Bipolar Disorder, Schizophrenia, Anxiety & OCD (DSM-5-TR criteria).

### 3. Top Recommended Resources
* **The Internal Medicine Bible:** *Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine* (Vols 1 & 2).
* **Clinical Examination Classic:** *Macleod's Clinical Examination* by Innes et al.
* **Pediatrics Standard:** *Nelson Textbook of Pediatrics* by Kliegman et al.
* **Rapid Clinical Pocket Guide:** *Oxford Handbook of Clinical Medicine*.

- [ ] Can you differentiate ischemic vs hemorrhagic stroke using non-contrast CT and determine tPA thrombolysis eligibility?
