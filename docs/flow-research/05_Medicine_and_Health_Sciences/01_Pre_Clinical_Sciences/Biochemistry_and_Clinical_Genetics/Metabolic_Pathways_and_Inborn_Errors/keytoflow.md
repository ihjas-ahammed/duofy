## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
**Enzymatic Control Points, Energy Coupling & Inborn Errors of Metabolism: Reciprocal Regulation of Glycolysis and Gluconeogenesis via the Bifunctional Enzyme PFK-2/FBPase-2 and Allosteric Master Regulator Fructose-2,6-Bisphosphate (F-2,6-BP activates PFK-1 and inhibits FBPase-1 under insulin drive; glucagon PKA phosphorylation lowers F-2,6-BP to drive gluconeogenesis), Glycogen Storage Diseases (Von Gierke Type I Glucose-6-Phosphatase deficiency $\to$ severe fasting hypoglycemia, lactic acidosis, hepatomegaly; Pompe Type II Lysosomal Acid Maltase $\to$ fatal infantile hypertrophic cardiomyopathy; McArdle Type V Skeletal Muscle Glycogen Phosphorylase $\to$ painful exercise cramps, myoglobinuria), Inborn Errors of Carbohydrate Metabolism (Hereditary Fructose Intolerance Aldolase B defect trapping intracellular inorganic phosphate $P_i$ as Fructose-1-P $\to$ ATP depletion, hypoglycemia, liver failure; Classic Galactosemia GALT defect $\to$ galactitol cataracts, liver failure, *E. coli* sepsis), and Urea Cycle Inborn Errors (Ornithine Transcarbamylase OTC Deficiency X-linked defect causing hyperammonemia with elevated urinary orotic acid vs CPS-I deficiency without orotic aciduria)** govern clinical medical genetics, pediatric metabolic emergencies, newborn screening programs, and biochemical diagnostics (David L. Nelson, Michael M. Cox *Lehninger Principles of Biochemistry* 8th ed.; Denise R. Ferrier *Lippincott Illustrated Reviews: Biochemistry* 7th ed.; Victor W. Rodwell et al. *Harper's Illustrated Biochemistry* 32nd ed.):
* **1. Glycolysis vs Gluconeogenesis Reciprocal F-2,6-BP Master Switch:**
  $$\mathbf{\text{Fed State (Insulin)} \to \text{Dephosphorylates PFK-2/FBPase-2} \to \uparrow \mathbf{\text{F-2,6-BP}} \implies \begin{cases} \mathbf{\uparrow \text{PFK-1 (Accelerates Glycolysis)}} \\ \mathbf{\downarrow \text{FBPase-1 (Inhibits Gluconeogenesis)}} \end{cases}}$$
  $$\mathbf{\text{Fasted State (Glucagon)} \to \uparrow \text{PKA} \to \text{Phosphorylates PFK-2/FBPase-2} \to \downarrow \mathbf{\text{F-2,6-BP}} \implies \begin{cases} \mathbf{\downarrow \text{PFK-1 (Halts Glycolysis)}} \\ \mathbf{\uparrow \text{FBPase-1 (Drives Gluconeogenesis)}} \end{cases}}$$
* **2. Glycogen Storage Diseases (GSD) Key Enzyme Matrix:**
  $$\mathbf{\text{Type I (Von Gierke): Glucose-6-Phosphatase} \ \Big| \ \text{Type II (Pompe): Lysosomal } \alpha\text{-1,4-Glucosidase (Acid Maltase)} \ \Big| \ \text{Type V (McArdle): Muscle Glycogen Phosphorylase}}$$
* **3. Carbohydrate Inborn Errors Energy Trapping Formulation:**
  $$\mathbf{\text{Fructose} \xrightarrow{\text{Fructokinase}} \text{Fructose-1-P } \xleftarrow{\text{Aldolase B Defect}} \implies \text{Traps Free } P_i \to \text{Depletes ATP} \to \text{Halts Gluconeogenesis (Severe Hypoglycemia)}}}$$
* **4. Urea Cycle Defects Diagnostic Formulation:**
  $$\mathbf{\text{OTC Deficiency (X-Linked): Hyperammonemia } + \mathbf{\uparrow \text{Urinary Orotic Acid}} \quad \Big| \quad \text{CPS-I Deficiency: Hyperammonemia WITHOUT Orotic Aciduria}}$$

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. Inborn Errors of Metabolism Problem-Solving Spectrum
* The F-2,6-BP "Bifunctional Switch" $\to$
  - PFK-2 and FBPase-2 exist on a single polypeptide chain with two functional domains.
  - **Fasted State:** Glucagon $\to \uparrow cAMP \to \uparrow PKA \implies$ **Phosphorylates the enzyme**. Phosphorylation **activates FBPase-2** and inactivates PFK-2. This degrades F-2,6-BP, releasing the brake on gluconeogenic **FBPase-1** and shutting down glycolytic **PFK-1**.
  - **Fed State:** Insulin $\to \uparrow \text{Protein Phosphatase-1} \implies$ **Dephosphorylates the enzyme**. Dephosphorylation **activates PFK-2** and inactivates FBPase-2. This synthesizes high F-2,6-BP, activating **PFK-1** to consume glucose.
* Glycogen Storage Disease Differential $\to$
  - **Von Gierke (Type I):** Defective Glucose-6-Phosphatase prevents both glycogenolysis and gluconeogenesis from releasing free glucose $\implies$ Severe fasting hypoglycemia, lactic acidosis (pyruvate shunted to lactate), hepatomegaly (glycogen and fat accumulation), hyperuricemia (gout).
  - **Pompe (Type II):** Acid maltase defect in lysosomes $\implies$ Glycogen accumulates inside lysosomes, damaging cardiac muscle cells $\implies$ Massive cardiomegaly, heart failure, hypotonia ("Pompe trashes the pump").
  - **McArdle (Type V):** Skeletal muscle cannot break down glycogen during anaerobic exercise $\implies$ Painful muscle cramping, myoglobinuria (rhabdomyolysis / red urine), "second-wind phenomenon" as liver gluconeogenesis delivers blood glucose.
* Urea Cycle vs Pyrimidine Synthesis Shunt $\to$
  - In Ornithine Transcarbamylase (OTC) deficiency, mitochondrial Carbamoyl Phosphate accumulates behind the block.
  - Excess carbamoyl phosphate leaks into the cytoplasm, entering the **pyrimidine synthesis pathway**, driving massive overproduction and excretion of **Orotic Acid**.
