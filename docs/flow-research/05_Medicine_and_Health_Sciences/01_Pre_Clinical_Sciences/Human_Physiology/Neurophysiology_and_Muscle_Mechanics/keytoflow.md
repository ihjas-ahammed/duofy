## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. Neurophysiology & Muscle Mechanics Problem-Solving Spectrum
* The Nernst Balance of Forces $\to$
  - The Nernst potential is the exact electrical voltage ($mV$) needed to prevent the net chemical diffusion of an ion down its concentration gradient.
  - Because $K^+$ is highly concentrated inside cells ($140\text{ mM}$ vs $4\text{ mM}$ outside), $K^+$ leaks out, leaving negative anions behind until the interior reaches $-90\text{ mV}$ ($E_{K^+}$).
  - Because $Na^+$ is concentrated outside ($145\text{ mM}$ vs $12\text{ mM}$ inside), $Na^+$ rushes inward during the action potential upstroke, driving membrane potential toward $+65\text{ mV}$ ($E_{Na^+}$).
* Malignant Hyperthermia $\to$
  - Mutation in the **RyR1 (Ryanodine Receptor)** gene triggered by volatile inhaled anesthetics (Halothane, Sevoflurane) or succinylcholine $\implies$ Uncontrolled, massive $Ca^{2+}$ efflux from SR into cytoplasm $\implies$ Sustained muscle rigidity, hypercatabolism, lactic acidosis, and lethal hyperthermia. Antidote: **Dantrolene** (RyR1 receptor antagonist)!
* Crossbridge ATP Kinetics & Rigor Mortis $\to$
  - ATP binding to the myosin head is required to **break the crossbridge detachment** from actin.
  - In death, cellular ATP depletion prevents detachment, locking myosin heads onto actin in permanent contraction (**Rigor Mortis**).
