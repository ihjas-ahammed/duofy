# Key to Flow: Surgery and Surgical Subspecialties

**Subject Area:** `05_Medicine_and_Health_Sciences / 04_Surgery_and_Surgical_Subspecialties`

---

## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
Surgery manages diseases, injuries, and deformities through operative manual and instrumental techniques, perioperative management, critical care, and wound healing.

---

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. The Surgical Core
1. **General Surgery:** Acute Abdomen (Appendicitis, Cholecystitis, Bowel Obstruction, Perforated Peptic Ulcer), Hernias (Inguinal vs Femoral), Thyroid & Breast Pathology.
2. **Preoperative & Postoperative Care:** Fluid resuscitation (Parkland formula for burns), Wound healing phases, Surgical site infection (SSI) prevention.
3. **Surgical Subspecialties:**
  * **Orthopedics:** Fractures (Salter-Harris classification), Dislocations, Compartment Syndrome (Emergency fasciotomy!), Arthroplasty.
  * **Neurosurgery:** Epidural vs Subdural Hematoma (CT: Convex lens vs Crescent shape), Elevated Intracranial Pressure (Cushing's Triad).
  * **Obstetrics & Gynecology:** Antenatal care, Preeclampsia/Eclampsia, Ectopic Pregnancy, C-Section, Cervical/Ovarian Cancer.
  * **Ophthalmology & ENT:** Cataract (Phacoemulsification), Glaucoma, Otitis Media, Tonsillitis, Tracheostomy.
  * **Anesthesiology:** Airway management (Mallampati score, Endotracheal intubation), Spinal vs Epidural anesthesia, General Anesthesia agents (Propofol, Sevoflurane, Succinylcholine).

### 2. Top Recommended Resources
* **The General Surgery Bible:** *Bailey & Love's Short Practice of Surgery*.
* **US Surgery Classic:** *Sabiston Textbook of Surgery*.
* **Obstetrics & Gynecology Classic:** *Williams Obstetrics*.
* **Orthopedics Standard:** *Apley & Solomon's System of Orthopaedics and Trauma*.
