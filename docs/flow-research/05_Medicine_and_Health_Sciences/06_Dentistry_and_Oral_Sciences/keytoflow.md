# Key to Flow: Dentistry and Oral Sciences

**Subject Area:** `05_Medicine_and_Health_Sciences / 06_Dentistry_and_Oral_Sciences`

---

## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
Dentistry studies the diagnosis, prevention, and treatment of conditions of the oral cavity, teeth, gums, maxilla, and mandible.

---

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. Key Dental Specialties
* **Conservative Dentistry & Endodontics:** Dental Caries (Streptococcus mutans), Pulpitis, Root Canal Treatment (RCT).
* **Periodontics:** Gingivitis, Periodontitis, Plaque & Calculus removal, Periodontal ligament.
* **Prosthodontics:** Crowns, Bridges, Dentures, Dental Implants (Osseointegration).
* **Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery:** Tooth extraction, Wisdom tooth impaction, Jaw fracture fixation, Orthognathic surgery.
* **Orthodontics:** Malocclusion (Class I, II, III), Braces, Clear Aligners.

### 2. Top Recommended Resources
* **Endodontics Bible:** *Cohen's Pathways of the Pulp* by Hargreaves & Berman.
* **Periodontics Classic:** *Carranza's Clinical Periodontology*.
* **Oral Surgery Classic:** *Contemporary Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery* by Hupp et al.
