## 🎯 Pedagogical Blueprint & Objective

## 🤖 LLM Prompt Generation Rules & Directives

1. **Slide 1 (`theory`):** State Classic Translocations in Neoplasia (Robbins 2021; Weinberg 2022):
   - **Translocation Concordance Formulations:**
     $$\mathbf{t(9;22) \to \text{BCR-ABL1 (CML / Imatinib)} \quad \Big| \quad t(8;14) \to \text{c-MYC (Burkitt Lymphoma)}}$$
     $$\mathbf{t(14;18) \to \text{BCL-2 (Follicular Lymphoma)} \quad \Big| \quad t(15;17) \to \text{PML-RARA (APL / ATRA)}}$$
     $$\mathbf{t(11;14) \to \text{Cyclin D1 (Mantle Cell)} \quad \Big| \quad t(11;22) \to \text{EWSR1-FLI1 (Ewing Sarcoma)}}$$
   - **The ATRA Invariant:**
     - In $t(15;17)$ APL, leukemic promyelocytes are locked in an undifferentiated state;
     - Pharmacological ATRA binds the PML-RARA fusion protein, forcing blasts to mature into benign, non-dividing granulocytes!
2. **Slide 2 (`ordering`):** Provide 5 steps of the targeted inhibition of BCR-ABL1 by Imatinib: (1) $t(9;22)$ translocation creates a chimeric BCR-ABL1 oncoprotein with constitutive tyrosine kinase activity, (2) hyperactive BCR-ABL1 continuously autophosphorylates and activates downstream STAT5, AKT, and RAS pathways in myeloid progenitors, (3) malignant granulocytes proliferate unchecked in bone marrow, causing Chronic Myelogenous Leukemia (CML), (4) Imatinib selectively enters the ATP-binding pocket of the BCR-ABL1 kinase domain in its inactive conformation, (5) blocking ATP binding abolishes tyrosine kinase substrate phosphorylation, halting leukemic proliferation and inducing selective apoptosis of CML clones!
3. **Slide 3 (`matching`):** Pair 4 concepts (Philadelphia Chromosome t(9;22), Burkitt Lymphoma t(8;14), Follicular Lymphoma t(14;18), Ewing Sarcoma t(11;22)) with their definitions.
4. **Slide 4 (`fill_in_blank`):** Single sentence blank testing recall of RARA (or Retinoic Acid Receptor Alpha). Plain text only.
5. **Slide 5 (`quiz`):** Quiz on Acute Promyelocytic Leukemia: A 28-year-old female presents with severe fatigue, petechiae, gum bleeding, and epistaxis. CBC reveals pancytopenia with abundant abnormal promyelocytes filled with stacked crystalline Auer rods. Coagulation panel shows evidence of acute Disseminated Intravascular Coagulation (DIC). Cytogenetic analysis confirms a $t(15;17)(q24;q21)$ translocation. What is the diagnosis, and how does the targeted first-line therapy (ATRA) achieve complete remission?
   - Auer rods in promyelocytes + DIC + $t(15;17) \implies$ **Acute Promyelocytic Leukemia (APL)**;
   - Molecular defect: **PML-RARA** fusion gene;
   - Mechanism of ATRA: Binds PML-RARA and **forces malignant promyelocytes to differentiate into mature granulocytes**;
   - What is the diagnosis and therapy mechanism? (**Acute Promyelocytic Leukemia (APL)** treated with **All-Trans Retinoic Acid (ATRA)**, which **induces terminal differentiation** of promyelocytes).
