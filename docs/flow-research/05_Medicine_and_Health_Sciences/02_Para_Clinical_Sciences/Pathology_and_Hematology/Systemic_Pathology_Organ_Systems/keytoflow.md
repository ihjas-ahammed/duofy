## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. Systemic Pathology Problem-Solving Spectrum
* Day 3-7 Post-MI Mechanical Rupture Anatomy $\to$
  - Macrophages secrete hydrolytic collagenases and phagocytose necrotic myocardium, making the tissue structurally weakest between **Days 3 and 7**.
  - **Free Wall Rupture:** Hemopericardium $\to$ rapid pericardial pressure rise $\to$ **Cardiac Tamponade** (Beck's triad: hypotension, JVD, muffled heart sounds, pulsus paradoxus).
  - **Papillary Muscle Rupture:** Posteromedial papillary muscle has single blood supply from RCA (posterior descending artery) $\implies$ rupture causes flail mitral leaflet and sudden **severe holosystolic murmur with acute pulmonary edema**.
* Podocyte Effacement & Negative Charge Loss $\to$
  - In Minimal Change Disease, cytokines efface podocyte foot processes and neutralize the negative polyanionic charge (heparan sulfate) of the glomerular basement membrane.
  - This allows **selective albuminuria** (albumin is negative) with profound hypoalbuminemia, generalized pitting edema, and secondary hyperlipidemia.
* Chronic Bronchitis vs Emphysema $\to$
  - **Chronic Bronchitis ("Blue Bloater"):** Submucosal gland hypertrophy ($\text{Reid Index} > 50\%$) produces copious mucus, airway plugging, chronic hypoxemia, cyanosis, and cor pulmonale.
  - **Emphysema ("Pink Puffer"):** Elastase excess degrades alveolar septa, reducing surface area for diffusion; hyperventilation maintains oxygenation until late stage, causing barrel chest and pursed-lip breathing.
