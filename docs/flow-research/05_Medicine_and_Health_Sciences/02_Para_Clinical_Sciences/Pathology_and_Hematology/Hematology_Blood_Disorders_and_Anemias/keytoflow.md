## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. Hematology & Coagulation Problem-Solving Spectrum
* Ferritin vs TIBC Physiological Logic $\to$
  - **Ferritin** reflects total intracellular iron storage in macrophages and hepatocytes.
  - **TIBC (Total Iron Binding Capacity)** is a direct measure of circulating **Transferrin**.
  - In iron deficiency, the body is iron-starved $\implies$ liver upregulates transferrin synthesis ($\uparrow \text{TIBC}$) to scavenge any available iron, while storage ferritin is depleted ($\downarrow \text{Ferritin}$).
  - In chronic disease, IL-6 stimulates liver production of **Hepcidin**, which degrades ferroportin $\implies$ iron is trapped inside macrophages ($\uparrow \text{Ferritin}$), and transferrin synthesis is suppressed ($\downarrow \text{TIBC}$).
* Subacute Combined Degeneration (SCD) $\to$
  - Vitamin $B_{12}$ is an essential cofactor for **Methylmalonyl-CoA Mutase** (converting methylmalonyl-CoA to succinyl-CoA).
  - $B_{12}$ deficiency causes accumulation of **Methylmalonic Acid (MMA)**, producing abnormal fatty acid synthesis and myelin breakdown in the dorsal columns (loss of proprioception/vibration) and lateral corticospinal tracts (spastic paresis).
  - Administering folate to a $B_{12}$-deficient patient corrects the macrocytic anemia but **worsens the irreversible neurological damage**!
* Hemophilia vs von Willebrand Disease (vWD) $\to$
  - **Hemophilia A:** X-linked recessive Factor VIII deficiency $\implies$ Deep tissue bleeding (hemarthroses into knees/elbows), normal bleeding time, **isolated prolonged aPTT**.
  - **von Willebrand Disease:** Autosomal dominant deficiency of vWF $\implies$ Mucocutaneous bleeding (epistaxis, menorrhagia, bruising), **prolonged Bleeding Time** (defective platelet adhesion via GpIb), and mildly prolonged aPTT (vWF stabilizes Factor VIII).
