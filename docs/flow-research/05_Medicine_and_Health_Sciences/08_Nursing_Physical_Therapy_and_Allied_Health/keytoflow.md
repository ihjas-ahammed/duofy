# Key to Flow: Nursing, Physical Therapy, and Allied Health

**Subject Area:** `05_Medicine_and_Health_Sciences / 08_Nursing_Physical_Therapy_and_Allied_Health`

---

## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
Nursing and Allied Health disciplines deliver direct patient care, rehabilitation, physical therapy, occupational therapy, and healthcare system administration.

---

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. Key Allied Health Disciplines
* **Nursing Science:**
  * **Nursing Process (ADPIE):** Assessment, Diagnosis, Planning, Implementation, Evaluation.
  * **Critical Care Nursing:** Triage, IV administration, Vital signs monitoring, Wound care.
* **Physiotherapy & Rehabilitation:**
  * **Neurological Rehab:** Stroke rehabilitation (Bobath technique, PNF), Spinal Cord Injury.
  * **Orthopedic Rehab:** Range of Motion (ROM), Isometric/Isotonic exercises, Gait analysis.
* **Occupational Therapy & Speech Therapy:** Activities of Daily Living (ADL) retraining, Dysphagia management.

### 2. Top Recommended Resources
* **Nursing Fundamentals:** *Potter & Perry's Fundamentals of Nursing*.
* **Physical Therapy Classic:** *Physical Rehabilitation* by Susan B. O'Sullivan.
* **NCLEX Board Review:** *Saunders Comprehensive Review for the NCLEX-RN Examination*.
