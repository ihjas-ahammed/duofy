# Key to Flow: Agronomy and Crop Science

**Subject Area:** `06_Agriculture_and_Environmental_Sciences / 01_Agronomy_and_Crop_Science`

---

## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
Agronomy and Crop Science study field crop production, crop physiology, seed technology, weed management, and plant breeding to maximize food yield sustainably.

---

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. Key Agricultural Pillars
* **Crop Production:** Cereals (Rice, Wheat, Maize), Pulses (Chickpea, Lentil), Oilseeds (Mustard, Soybean), Cash Crops (Cotton, Sugarcane).
* **Plant Breeding & Genetics:** Hybrid seed production, Heterosis (Hybrid vigor), Marker-Assisted Selection (MAS).
* **Weed & Pest Management:** Integrated Pest Management (IPM), Selective vs Non-selective Herbicides.

### 2. Top Recommended Resources
* **Agronomy Standard:** *Principles of Crop Production* by George Acquaah.
* **Plant Breeding Classic:** *Principles of Plant Genetics and Breeding* by George Acquaah.
