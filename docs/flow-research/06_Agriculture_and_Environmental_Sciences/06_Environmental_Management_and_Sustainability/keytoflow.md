# Key to Flow: Environmental Management and Sustainability

**Subject Area:** `06_Agriculture_and_Environmental_Sciences / 06_Environmental_Management_and_Sustainability`

---

## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
Environmental Management develops sustainable strategies for ecosystem conservation, waste reduction, climate change adaptation, and Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA).

---

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. Key Sustainability Tools
* **Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA):** Scoping, Baseline studies, Impact prediction, Public consultation, Environmental Management Plan (EMP).
* **Waste Management:** Circular economy, Composting, Anaerobic digestion, Hazardous waste disposal.
* **Climate Resilience:** Carbon sequestration, Regenerative agriculture, Watershed management.

### 2. Top Recommended Resources
* **EIA Classic:** *Methods of Environmental Impact Assessment* by Peter Morris & Riki Therivel.
* **Sustainability Standard:** *Sustainability Science* by Harald Heinrichs et al.
