# Key to Flow: Animal Husbandry and Veterinary Science

**Subject Area:** `06_Agriculture_and_Environmental_Sciences / 04_Animal_Husbandry_and_Veterinary_Science`

---

## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
Animal Husbandry and Veterinary Science study livestock breeding, animal nutrition, dairy science, poultry production, and veterinary medical treatment.

---

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. Key Livestock Branches
* **Ruminant Nutrition:** Rumen microbiology (Cellulose digestion via volatile fatty acids: Acetate, Propionate, Butyrate).
* **Dairy & Poultry Tech:** Milk processing (Pasteurization, Homogenization), Broiler & Layer poultry production.
* **Veterinary Medicine:** Infectious livestock diseases (Foot-and-Mouth disease, Mastitis, Anthrax, Avian Influenza).

### 2. Top Recommended Resources
* **Animal Nutrition Classic:** *Animal Nutrition* by Peter McDonald et al.
* **Veterinary Medicine Classic:** *Merck Veterinary Manual*.

---
