# Key to Flow: Horticulture and Forestry

**Subject Area:** `06_Agriculture_and_Environmental_Sciences / 03_Horticulture_and_Forestry`

---

## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
Horticulture and Forestry study the cultivation of fruits (Pomology), vegetables (Olericulture), ornamental plants (Floriculture), and forest management (Silviculture).

---

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. Key Branches
* **Pomology:** Fruit crops (Mango, Citrus, Apple, Banana). Propagation (Grafting, Budding, Layering).
* **Olericulture:** Vegetable production, Protected cultivation (Greenhouses, Polyhouses).
* **Floriculture & Landscaping:** Flower crops (Rose, Orchid, Chrysanthemum), Landscape gardening.
* **Silviculture:** Forest regeneration, Agroforestry systems, Timber management.

### 2. Top Recommended Resources
* **Horticulture Classic:** *Introductory Horticulture* by Carroll Shry & H. Edward Reiley.
* **Forestry Classic:** *Introduction to Forestry Science* by L. DeVere Burton.
