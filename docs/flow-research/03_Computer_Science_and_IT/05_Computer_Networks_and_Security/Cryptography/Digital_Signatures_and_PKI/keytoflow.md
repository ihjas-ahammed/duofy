## 📌 Core Concept & Mental Model
**Non-Repudiation & The Hash-then-Sign Paradigm, Probabilistic Signature Padding (RSA-PSS), The ECDSA Nonce $k$ Secret Invariant & The Sony PS3 Catastrophe, Deterministic Ed25519 Signatures (RFC 8032), and The Hierarchical X.509 Public Key Infrastructure (Certificate Transparency & OCSP Stapling)** govern digital authentication and asymmetric identity trust (Mihir Bellare & Phillip Rogaway 1996 RSA-PSS; NIST FIPS 186-4 ECDSA; Daniel J. Bernstein et al. 2012 Ed25519; ITU-T X.509; RFC 6960 OCSP; RFC 6962 CT; Katz & Lindell Chapter 12–13; Serious Cryptography Chapter 13):
* **1. Digital Signature Foundations:**
  - **The 3 Cryptographic Guarantees:**
    1. **Authenticity:** Verifies the message was generated by the holder of private key $d$.
    2. **Integrity:** Guarantees message $M$ was not altered in transit.
    3. **Non-Repudiation:** Signer cannot deny creating the signature in a court of law.
  - **Hash-then-Sign Paradigm:** Sign the fixed-length hash $H(M)$ rather than large message $M$:
    $$\mathbf{\sigma = \text{Sign}_{SK}(H(M)) \qquad ; \qquad \text{Verify}_{PK}(M, \sigma) \in \{\text{True}, \text{False}\}}$$
* **2. RSA-PSS vs Textbook RSA Signatures (RFC 8017):**
  - **Textbook RSA Signature Flaw:** $\sigma = H(M)^d \pmod n$ is multiplicatively malleable ($\sigma_1 \cdot \sigma_2 = (H(M_1)H(M_2))^d = \text{Sign}(H(M_1)H(M_2))$) and vulnerable to existential forgery.
  - **RSA-PSS (Probabilistic Signature Scheme):** Introduces random salt and Mask Generation Functions (MGF1), achieving provable security in the Random Oracle Model (EUF-CMA: Existential Unforgeability under Chosen-Message Attack).
* **3. ECDSA & The Catastrophic Nonce $k$ Reuse Vulnerability (FIPS 186-4):**
  - **Signing:** Choose secret random scalar $nonce \ k \in [1, n-1]$.
    $$\mathbf{R = k \cdot G = (r_x, r_y) \implies \mathbf{r = r_x \pmod n} \qquad ; \qquad \mathbf{s \equiv k^{-1}\big(H(M) + d \cdot r\big) \pmod n}}$$
    - Signature is the pair $\mathbf{(r, s)}$.
  - **The Sony PS3 Disaster (2010):**
    - If a signer reuses the exact same nonce $k$ to sign two distinct messages $M_1, M_2$, $r$ is identical!
    - An attacker solves for $k$ using elementary algebra:
      $$s_1 - s_2 = k^{-1}\big(H(M_1) - H(M_2)\big) \implies \mathbf{k = \frac{H(M_1) - H(M_2)}{s_1 - s_2} \pmod n}$$
    - Once $k$ is known, the **master private signing key $d$ is recovered instantly**: $\mathbf{d = \frac{s_1 k - H(M_1)}{r} \pmod n}$!
* **4. Ed25519 & Deterministic Signatures (RFC 8032):**
  - Daniel J. Bernstein invented Ed25519 over Twisted Edwards curve $-x^2 + y^2 = 1 - \frac{121665}{121666}x^2 y^2$.
  - **Deterministic Nonce Derivation:** $\mathbf{k = \text{SHA-512}(\text{PrivateSeed} \ || \ M) \pmod \ell}$.
  - **Immunity:** Eliminates faulty random number generators; nonce reuse is mathematically impossible!
* **5. X.509 Public Key Infrastructure (PKI):**
  - **Chain of Trust:** $\text{Root CA (Self-Signed in OS Store)} \xrightarrow{\text{Signs}} \text{Intermediate CA} \xrightarrow{\text{Signs}} \text{Leaf Server Certificate}$.
  - **Revocation:** **OCSP Stapling (RFC 6960)** caches time-stamped CA signatures on the web server; **Certificate Transparency (CT Logs - RFC 6962)** publicly audits all issued certificates in append-only Merkle Trees.

## 🔑 Best Known Study Methodologies & Learning Framework

### 1. The Signature & Trust Evolution Flow
* Theory $\to$ Hash-then-Sign + Non-Repudiation.
* RSA Evolution $\to$ Textbook (Malleable) $\to$ RSA-PSS (Salted EUF-CMA).
* Elliptic Evolution $\to$ ECDSA (Vulnerable Nonce $k$) $\to$ Ed25519 (Deterministic $k$).
* Public Key Trust $\to$ X.509 CA Chain + OCSP Stapling + Certificate Transparency.
