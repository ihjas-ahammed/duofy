## What's new in 26.7.15

A big one — a fresh look, smarter lessons, and in-app updates so you never have to sideload by hand again.

### ✨ New look & feel
- **Celestial theme** with full **light + dark mode** support across every screen.
- Rebranded app **icon** and polished navigation, cards, and slides.
- **Confetti burst and XP count-up** when you finish a lesson.

### 🚀 Auto-update
- The app now **checks GitHub for new builds** and shows you exactly *what's new*.
- One tap to **download and install** — no more manual APK sharing.

### 🧠 Smarter learning
- **Metacognitive lesson loop** with four new exercise types.
- **Auto-Next** flow moves you straight into the next lesson.
- **AI cross-check** verification for more reliable questions and answers.
- **Learner profile v2** and a personalized writing-style survey in Settings.

### 🎯 Deadlines & progress
- **Target section deadlines** with cumulative, sequential targets.
- Dynamic, page-scaled **lesson time estimates**.
- Layout-based **course progress bars** and a glassy targets button.

### 📚 Content & setup
- **Onboarding walkthrough**, advanced mode, next-up hero card, and daily goal.
- **Deterministic contents-to-pages mapping** and **bulk syllabus upload**.

### 🔒 Reliability & security
- API keys moved to **auth-gated, secured Firestore** storage.
- Auto-resolving **sync conflicts**, plus fixes for blank-screen and dead-end bugs.
- The app now also builds and runs on the **web**.
